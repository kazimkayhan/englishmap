# Practice Plans

**All stages** · Daily templates + weekly rotation

---

## Learning goals

1. Use **30- and 45-minute** daily blocks effectively
2. **Rotate** all six skills weekly
3. Follow **busy-week minimum** (15 min)
4. Connect to **12 / 24 / 52-week** plans in [study-plans/](study-plans/)
5. Never wonder *“What should I study today?”*

---

## Why this skill matters

A plan removes decision fatigue. When life is heavy, **open the plan and start** — that is half the battle.

---

## Level focus

Templates adapt by stage — notes below each table.

## Daily template — 30 minutes

| Min | Skill | Activity |
|-----|-------|----------|
| 5 | Vocabulary | Review + 5 new words |
| 10 | Grammar / Writing | One lesson + 3 sentences |
| 10 | Listening | One clip + dictation |
| 5 | Speaking | Read aloud / shadow |

**Stage 0:** Replace grammar with alphabet/words from [02-beginner-foundations.md](02-beginner-foundations.md).

---

## Daily template — 45 minutes

| Min | Skill | Activity |
|-----|-------|----------|
| 5 | Review | Yesterday’s words |
| 10 | Vocabulary | 10 new words + sentences |
| 10 | Grammar | Module + exercises |
| 10 | Listening | Clip + transcript check |
| 10 | Speaking / Writing | Alternate days |

---

## Busy-week minimum — 15 minutes

1. Review **10 words** (5 min)  
2. **Listen** to one short clip (5 min)  
3. **Speak** 5 sentences aloud (5 min)  

Log as ✓ in tracker — **habit preserved**.

## Three-block day (15 + 15 + 15)

| Block | When | What |
|-------|------|------|
| A | Morning | Words + pronunciation |
| B | Afternoon | Grammar + write 3 sentences |
| C | Evening | Listen + repeat aloud |

Use when you cannot study 45 minutes straight.

---

## Examples

**Monday 30 min (Stage 1):**

- 5 min: review *study, learn, book*  
- 10 min: present simple *she studies*  
- 10 min: slow YouTube clip (see resources)  
- 5 min: read dialogue aloud  

**Friday 45 min (Stage 2):**

- 5 min review  
- 10 min past tense story write  
- 10 min listen to 2-min clip  
- 10 min record 2-min opinion  
- 10 min fix grammar from recording

## Practice tasks

1. Choose 30 or 45 min template — commit 7 days.
2. Copy weekly rotation into your notes.
3. Open matching study-plan file for your week number.
4. Log each day in [16-tracker.md](16-tracker.md).

## Self-check questions

1. What are the four blocks in a 45-minute day?
2. What is the busy-week minimum?
3. Which day focuses on speaking in the rotation?
4. What week is beginner checkpoint?
5. Where is the full 52-week breakdown?

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Random study daily | Follow rotation |
| Skip speaking | Friday is speaking day — non-negotiable |
| No rest day | Sunday light or rest |
| Wrong level plan | Placement quiz first |
