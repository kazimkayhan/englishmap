# Core Grammar

**Stage 1** · Months 1–3 (with foundations)

---

## Learning goals

1. Use **verb be** (am, is, are) correctly
2. Use **have / has** for possession and facts
3. Build **present simple** sentences (habits and facts)
4. Ask **yes/no** and **wh-** questions
5. Use **negatives** (don't, doesn't, not)
6. Use **there is / there are**

---

## Why this skill matters

Grammar is the skeleton of English. With these patterns, you can describe your life, ask questions, and stop guessing word order.

> *Learn patterns, not single sentences. Swap one word — make a new sentence.*

---

## Level focus

**Stage 1** (A1–A2).

## Step-by-step lessons

### Lesson 1 — Subject + be (20 min)

| Subject | Be | Example |
|---------|-----|---------|
| I | am | I am tired. |
| You | you are | You are kind. |
| He / she / it | is | She is at home. |
| We / they | are | We are students. |

**Negative:** add **not**

- I am **not** busy.
- She is **not** here.

**Questions:** swap order

- **Are** you fine? → Yes, I am. / No, I am not.
- **Is** he home?

> **Dari note:** Dari often drops “to be” in present. In English, **do not drop it**: say *I am fine*, not *I fine*.

---

### Lesson 2 — Have / has (20 min)

Use **have** and **has** to show **possession** (I have a book) or some **states** (I have time / She has a problem).

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I / you / we / they | **have** | I have a phone. |
| He / she / it | **has** | She has a sister. |

**Negative:**

- I **do not have** (don't have) Wi-Fi today.
- He **does not have** (doesn't have) class on Friday.

**Questions:**

- **Do** you have a quiet room? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- **Does** she have your number? → Yes, she does.

**Contrast with *be*:**

- I **am** tired. (state — adjective)
- I **have** time. (possession / availability)
- She **is** a student. (identity)
- She **has** a book. (possession)

> **Dari note:** داشتن (*dâštan* — to have) works differently. English uses **have/has** in present, not a separate verb like *am/is*.

**Pattern practice:**

- I have + noun → I have a notebook.
- She has + noun → She has courage.
- Do you have + noun? → Do you have internet?

---

### Lesson 3 — Present simple (20 min)

Use for **habits** and **facts**.

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I / you / we / they | study | I study English. |
| He / she / it | studies (+s) | She **studies** at home. |

**Negative:**

- I **do not** (don't) study at night.
- He **does not** (doesn't) study at night.

**Questions:**

- **Do** you study English? → Yes, I do.
- **Does** she study English? → Yes, she does.

---

### Lesson 4 — Wh- questions (15 min)

| Word | Asks about |
|------|------------|
| What | thing |
| Where | place |
| When | time |
| Who | person |
| Why | reason |
| How | way |

- **What** do you study? → I study English.
- **Where** do you live?
- **How** do you learn? → I learn on my phone.

---

### Lesson 5 — There is / there are (15 min)

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| There is + singular | There is a book on the table. |
| There are + plural | There are two windows. |

**Negative:** There is **not** / There are **not** (no)

**Question:** **Is** there a phone here?

---

### Lesson 6 — Articles a / an / the (20 min)

| Article | Use |
|---------|-----|
| a | one general thing: a book |
| an | before vowel sound: an apple |
| the | specific thing: the book (we know which one) |

**Examples:**

- I need **a** pen.
- She reads **an** article.
- **The** internet is slow today.

> **Dari note:** Dari has no *a/the*. Practice **out loud** with every new noun.

---

### Lesson 7 — Object pronouns and possessives (15 min)

| Subject | Object | Possessive |
|---------|--------|------------|
| I | me | my |
| you | you | your |
| he | him | his |
| she | her | her |
| we | us | our |
| they | them | their |

- I help **her**. **Her** book is new.
- **Our** family is kind.

---

## Pattern practice (swap words)

**Pattern:** I + verb + noun

- I study English.
- I read books.
- I drink tea.

**Pattern:** She + verb-s + noun

- She works at home.
- She learns online.

---

## Examples

**Daily routine:**

> I wake up at six. I have tea and bread. I study English for thirty minutes. My sister has a phone; she works at home. We have lunch together. In the evening, I read messages on my phone. I do not have much free time, but I have hope.

**Dialogue — At home:**

> **A:** Do you have a quiet room for study?  
> **B:** Yes, I have a small room. I do not have a desk, but I have a chair and my phone.  
> **A:** Does your sister have English books?  
> **B:** She has one old book. We have the internet on good days.

**Questions for practice:**

> What do you do in the morning? Where does your family live? Is there a quiet room for study?

## Practice tasks

1. Write 10 sentences with *am/is/are*.
2. Write 8 sentences with *have/has* (affirmative, negative, question).
3. Write 5 questions with *do/does* (present simple verbs).
3. Write 5 sentences with *there is/are*.
4. Add *a/an/the* to 10 nouns.
5. Drills: **[exercises/03-grammar-drills.md](exercises/03-grammar-drills.md)**

## Self-check questions

1. When do we add **-s** to a verb?
2. Fix: “She don't like tea.”
3. Make a question from: “They live in Kabul.”
4. Choose: There (is / are) many books.
5. Why is “I am study” wrong?

## Common mistakes (Dari speakers)

| Mistake | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| I fine | I **am** fine |
| He go every day | He **goes** every day |
| I no like | I **do not** like |
| book on table (no article) | **a** book on **the** table |
