Use with [07-speaking.md](../07-speaking.md), [12-study-strategies.md](../12-study-strategies.md).

---

## Confidence is a skill

Confidence is not a gift. It is **practice + kind self-talk + proof of progress**.

> *You are allowed to sound like a learner. Learners become fluent.*

---

## 7 confidence rules

1. **Speak before you feel ready** — feeling follows action.  
2. **One mistake per correction** — not ten at once.  
3. **Record in private** — proof beats memory.  
4. **Compare to last month** — not to native speakers online.  
5. **Celebrate speaking days** — mark ✓ in tracker.  
6. **Rest without guilt** — return tomorrow.  
7. **Your voice has value** — ideas matter more than accent.

---

## Shame recovery script (read aloud)

> I stopped for a while. That is human. Today I study fifteen minutes. I am still a learner. I am still moving forward. I do not need permission to try again.

Write your own version in English. Read every Monday.

---

## Weekly confidence tasks

| Day | Task |
|-----|------|
| Mon | Write 1 thing you did well in English |
| Wed | Speak 5 sentences without reading |
| Fri | Record 60 sec — delete if you want after listening |
| Sun | Read tracker wins from the week |

---

## Safe speaking environments

| Safe | Less safe |
|------|-----------|
| Alone in room | Public criticism |
| Trusted sister | Strangers judging accent |
| Voice memo private folder | Posting recordings publicly without reason |

---

## When someone mocks your English

You may say: *I am learning.* Then leave the conversation mentally. Do not carry their words into study time.
