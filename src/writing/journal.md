# Journal Practice

**Learning goal:** Build writing fluency through daily journaling

---

## Why journal in English?

- Practice without judgment
- Track progress over time
- Apply new vocabulary immediately
- Build writing stamina
- Process emotions in target language

---

## Daily journal template (5-10 minutes)

1. **Date:** Today is [date]
2. **Feeling:** I feel [emotion] because...
3. **Did:** Today I [action]
4. **Learned:** I learned [new thing]
5. **Tomorrow:** Tomorrow I will [plan]

---

## Journal prompts

- Describe your morning routine
- Write about a challenge you overcame
- Describe a family member
- What are you grateful for?
- What is your dream for next year?

---

## Privacy & storage

- Keep in password-protected notes app
- Use code name if needed
- Delete or archive older entries
- Don't share unless you want to
