# Sentence Writing

**Learning goal:** Write clear, correct English sentences

---

## Basic sentence structure (SVO)

**Subject + Verb + Object**

- I study English.
- She reads books.
- We drink tea.

---

## Types of sentences

1. **Statement:** I am learning English.
2. **Question:** Do you study daily?
3. **Command:** Read this sentence.
4. **Exclamation:** I passed the test!

---

## Common sentence patterns

- Subject + be + adjective: I am tired.
- Subject + have + noun: She has a phone.
- Subject + verb + object: We eat breakfast.
- There + be + noun: There is hope.
