# Paragraph Writing

**Learning goal:** Organize sentences into clear paragraphs

---

## Paragraph structure

**5-8 sentences:**

1. **Topic sentence** (main idea)
2. **Supporting details** (2-4 sentences)
3. **Example** (optional)
4. **Concluding sentence**

---

## Topic sentence tips

- States the main idea clearly
- Usually first sentence
- Specific enough to guide paragraph
- Example: "Daily English practice improves skills faster than weekend marathons."

---

## Linking words between sentences

- **Addition:** also, moreover, furthermore
- **Example:** for example, for instance
- **Contrast:** however, on the other hand
- **Result:** therefore, as a result

---

## Practice

Write one paragraph (6-8 sentences) about why you study English.
