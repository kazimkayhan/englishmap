# Complex Grammar

**Learning goal:** Use advanced structures correctly

---

## Advanced grammar patterns

### 1. Relative clauses
- The woman **who teaches** online is very helpful.
- The book **which I read** was excellent.

### 2. Conditional sentences (all types)
- **Zero:** If you heat water, it boils.
- **First:** If I practice, I will improve.
- **Second:** If I had time, I would study more.
- **Third:** If I had started earlier, I would have progressed faster.

### 3. Passive voice
- English **is spoken** worldwide.
- The message **was sent** yesterday.

### 4. Reported speech
- Direct: She said, "I am studying."
- Reported: She said she was studying.

### 5. Subjunctive mood
- I suggest that she **study** harder. (not "studies")
- It's important that he **be** on time.
