# Academic English

**Learning goal:** Read and write academic texts

---

## Academic writing style

### Formal language
- Use third person: "Research shows..." (not "I think...")
- Avoid contractions: "do not" (not "don't")
- Use academic vocabulary: utilize, demonstrate, indicate

### Structure
- **Introduction:** Topic, thesis statement
- **Body paragraphs:** Point, evidence, explanation
- **Conclusion:** Summary, implications

### Citations
- Reference sources: According to Smith (2020)...
- In-text citations and bibliography

---

## Academic vocabulary

**Verbs:** analyze, evaluate, demonstrate, investigate, examine  
**Nouns:** methodology, framework, hypothesis, conclusion  
**Connectors:** furthermore, nevertheless, consequently

---

## Critical reading

1. Identify thesis and main arguments
2. Evaluate evidence quality
3. Note assumptions and biases
4. Compare with other sources

---

## Academic presentations

- Clear thesis
- Organized points
- Evidence from sources
- Formal delivery
