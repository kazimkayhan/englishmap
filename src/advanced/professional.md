# Professional English

**Learning goal:** Use English effectively in work contexts

---

## Professional communication skills

### Email writing
- **Subject:** Clear and specific
- **Greeting:** Dear Mr./Ms./Dr. [Last Name]
- **Purpose:** State in first paragraph
- **Details:** Bullet points if multiple items
- **Close:** Thank you for your time. / Best regards,

### Business vocabulary
- meeting, deadline, proposal, project
- collaborate, coordinate, implement
- stakeholder, objective, deliverable

### Professional phrases
- "I would like to..." (instead of "I want")
- "Could you please..." (polite request)
- "Thank you for your consideration."
- "I look forward to hearing from you."

---

## Presentations

**Structure:**
1. Introduction (purpose)
2. Main points (2-3)
3. Conclusion
4. Q&A

**Language:**
- "Today I will discuss..."
- "Moving on to..."
- "In conclusion..."
- "Are there any questions?"

---

## Professional meetings

- Arrive/join on time
- Introduce yourself clearly
- Take turns speaking
- Summarize action items at end
