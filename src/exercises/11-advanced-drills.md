## A — Relative clauses

1. The course ___ I take is free. (which/that)  
2. The student ___ helps me is my sister. (who)  
3. Combine: *This is a podcast. I listen to the podcast daily.*

---

## B — Conditionals

4. If she studies daily, she ___ (improve) — will  
5. If I had Wi-Fi, I ___ (download) more lessons. — would  
6. If they had started young, they ___ (learn) faster. — would have learned

## C — Passive

7. Many lessons ___ (record) online. (record)  
8. My homework ___ (finish) last night. (finish)
