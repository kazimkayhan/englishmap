# Checkpoint 1 Quiz (End of Beginner — Month 3)

**60 min** · No dictionary · Keys: [answer-keys/checkpoint-1-quiz-KEYS.md](../answer-keys/checkpoint-1-quiz-KEYS.md)

---

## Part A — Foundations (1–6)

1. Write alphabet A–M.  
2. She ___ a student. (am/is/are)  
3. I ___ a phone. (have/has)  
4. He ___ (study) every day.  
5. There ___ two books. (is/are)  
6. Write introduction (5 sentences).

---

## Part B — Vocabulary (7–10)

7. Write 20 words: topic *home*.  
8. Opposite of *sad*: ___  
9. Use *because* in one sentence.  
10. Write 5 words + one sentence each.

---

## Part C — Skills (11–15)

11. Dictation (teacher/self read aloud): *Thank you for your help.*  
12. Dictation: *I study English at home every day.*  
13. Reading 2 in [graded-readings.md](../appendices/graded-readings.md) — how long did she study?  
14. Write paragraph 60+ words: *My day*.  
15. Record 60-sec introduction (self-rubric).

---

## Part D — Review (16–20)

16. Fix: *She don't like tea.*  
17. Article: I ate ___ apple.  
18. Question: They live here. →  
19. Name 3 pronunciation sounds you practice.  
20. What is your study plan for month 4? (2 sentences, future)

**Pass:** 15/20 written + speaking rubric average 3+
