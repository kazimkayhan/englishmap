# Checkpoint 3 Quiz (Pre-advanced — Month 12)

**Time:** 60 minutes · Keys: [answer-keys/checkpoint-3-quiz-KEYS.md](../answer-keys/checkpoint-3-quiz-KEYS.md)

---

## Part A — Relative clauses (1–4)

1. The woman ___ teaches online is kind. (who/which)  
2. The book ___ I read was hard. (who/which/that)  
3. Combine: *I have a phone. The phone is old.* → ___  
4. Write one sentence with *who* or *which*.

---

## Part B — Conditionals (5–8)

5. If I practice, I ___ improve. (will)  
6. If I ___ (have) more time, I would study more.  
7. If she ___ (start) earlier, she would have learned more. (start — past perfect)  
8. Write one first conditional about English.

---

## Part C — Passive & formal (9–12)

9. English ___ (teach) in many countries.  
10. The email ___ (send) yesterday. (send)  
11. Formal: *I want help* → better: ___  
12. Write subject line for course inquiry email.

---

## Part D — Reading & writing (13–16)

13. Summarize Reading 4 in [appendices/graded-readings.md](../appendices/graded-readings.md) in 2 sentences.  
14. Hedge: soften *English is easy.* → ___  
15. Formal email 60+ words: request course information.  
16. List 5 hedging words.
