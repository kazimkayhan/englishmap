## Read this text

**A Quiet Morning**

Nadia wakes up at six. She drinks tea and opens her phone. She studies English for thirty minutes in a quiet room. Her sister sometimes listens and smiles. Today Nadia learns ten new words. She feels tired but proud. She believes small steps are important.

---

## A — Comprehension

1. What time does Nadia wake up?  
2. How long does she study English?  
3. Who sometimes listens?  
4. How many new words today?  
5. How does she feel?

---

## B — Inference

6. Does Nadia study alone always? (yes/no + one word why)  
7. Is her study serious? (yes/no)
