# Reading Strategies

**Learning goal:** Read more effectively and efficiently

---

## Core reading strategies

### Strategy 1: Preview before reading
Look at title, headings, first sentences to predict content.

### Strategy 2: Scan for specific information
Find names, dates, numbers quickly without reading everything.

### Strategy 3: Skim for main ideas
Read first and last sentences of paragraphs for gist.

### Strategy 4: Read actively
Underline, note questions, predict what comes next.

### Strategy 5: Guess from context
Use surrounding words to understand unknown vocabulary.

---

## Practice activities

1. Preview one article — write 3 predictions
2. Scan a text for 5 specific facts in 2 minutes
3. Skim a 500-word article, write main idea in one sentence
