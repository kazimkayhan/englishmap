# Graded Readings

**Learning goal:** Read texts at your level to build skills

---

## Reading levels

- **Level A:** 50-100 words, present tense, common words
- **Level B:** 100-200 words, simple past, basic descriptions
- **Level C:** 200-400 words, opinions, multiple tenses
- **Level D:** 400-800 words, arguments, complex sentences
- **Level E:** 800+ words, academic/professional content

---

## How to use graded readings

1. Choose text where you know ~80% of words
2. Read once without dictionary
3. Read again, guess unknown words
4. Check meanings of 3-5 key unknown words only
5. Write 2-3 sentence summary
