# Placement Quiz

**Find your starting level in 20 minutes**

This quiz helps you discover where to begin in EnglishMap. Try your best — **guessing is okay**, but don't look up answers or use translation tools.

---

## Instructions

1. **Do all 25 questions** (or as many as you can)
2. **Check your answers** in [Answer Keys](./answer-keys/guide.md) after finishing
3. **Count correct answers** and see your recommended starting point below
4. Be honest — placing yourself correctly saves time

---

- c) day

**7.** This is my _____ (place where I sleep).

## Section 3: Reading Comprehension (5 questions)

**Read this passage:**

> My name is Layla. I am 20 years old. I live in Kabul with my family. I study English at home every day for 30 minutes. I want to learn English because it will help me get a job online. My favorite subject is vocabulary. I listen to English podcasts and read simple articles. Sometimes it is difficult, but I do not give up.

**21.** Where does Layla live?
- a) Herat
- b) Kabul
- c) Kandahar

**22.** How long does Layla study English each day?
- a) 10 minutes
- b) 30 minutes
- c) 2 hours

**23.** Why does Layla want to learn English?
- a) To travel
- b) To get a job online
- c) To teach others

**24.** What is Layla's favorite English subject?
- a) Grammar
- b) Vocabulary
- c) Pronunciation

**25.** What does Layla say about difficulty?
- a) She gives up
- b) She asks for help
- c) She does not give up

## Scoring Guide

**Count your correct answers:**

| Score | Level | Start Here |
|-------|-------|------------|
| **0-8** | Pre-beginner / Beginner | [Beginner Foundations](./02-beginner-foundations.md) — Start with alphabet and first 100 words |
| **9-16** | High Beginner / Low Intermediate | [Core Grammar](./03-core-grammar.md) — Review beginner foundations quickly, then focus on grammar |

## Next Steps

### After Scoring

1. **Note your level** in your [Progress Tracker](./16-tracker.md)
2. **Start reading** the recommended chapter today
3. **Choose a study plan:**
   - 0-8 → [12-Week Beginner Plan](./plans/12-week.md)
   - 9-16 → [24-Week Intermediate Plan](./plans/24-week.md)
   - 17+ → [52-Week Mastery Path](./plans/52-week.md)
4. **Retake this quiz** every 8-12 weeks to measure progress

### If You Scored Lower Than Expected

**Don't worry!** This is your starting point, not your limit.
- Everyone starts somewhere
- Honest placement = faster progress
- Review is powerful — you'll move through early stages quickly

### If You Scored Higher Than Expected

**Great!**
- Skim earlier chapters to fill any gaps
- Start at your recommended level
- Adjust up if it feels too easy after 1-2 weeks
