# Be & Have

**Learning goal:** Master the two most important verbs in English

---

## Why these verbs matter

**Be** and **have** are the foundation of English grammar. You use them in almost every sentence. They work differently from other verbs, so learn them separately first.

> **Critical fact:** In Dari, you can drop "to be" in present tense. In English, **you cannot**. Always include am/is/are.

---

## Verb "be" (am, is, are)

### Forms in present tense

| Subject | Be form | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| I | am | I **am** tired. |
| you | are | You **are** kind. |
| he / she / it | is | She **is** at home. |
| we | are | We **are** students. |
| they | are | They **are** ready. |

### Three main uses

#### 1. Identity / occupation
- I **am** a student.
- She **is** a teacher.
- They **are** sisters.

#### 2. Description / state
- I **am** happy.
- The water **is** cold.
- We **are** tired.

#### 3. Location
- I **am** at home.
- He **is** in the room.
- They **are** here.

---

### Negative form: add "not"

| Subject | Negative | Short form | Example |
|---------|----------|------------|---------|
| I | am not | I'm not | I **am not** busy. / I**'m not** busy. |
| you | are not | aren't / you're not | You **are not** late. / You **aren't** late. |
| he/she/it | is not | isn't / he's not | She **is not** here. / She **isn't** here. |
| we | are not | aren't / we're not | We **are not** ready. / We **aren't** ready. |
| they | are not | aren't / they're not | They **are not** home. / They **aren't** home. |

**Note:** "Am not" has no common contraction like "amn't" — use "I'm not."

---

### Question form: swap subject and "be"

| Statement | Question | Answer |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| You **are** fine. | **Are** you fine? | Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. |
| She **is** at home. | **Is** she at home? | Yes, she is. / No, she isn't. |
| They **are** ready. | **Are** they ready? | Yes, they are. / No, they aren't. |

**Wh- questions:**
- **Where** are you?
- **Who** is she?
- **Why** are they late?

> **Dari tip:** In Dari questions, you can keep the same word order. In English, you MUST move the verb: "Are you ready?" not "You are ready?"

---

### Common "be" expressions (memorize these)

| Expression | Meaning | Example |
|------------|---------|---------|
| be careful | pay attention | **Be careful** with hot water. |
| be quiet | don't make noise | Please **be quiet** in class. |
| be patient | wait calmly | **Be patient** with yourself. |
| be ready | prepared | I **am ready** to start. |
| be sure | certain | I **am sure** it's correct. |
| be sorry | apologetic | I **am sorry** I'm late. |

## Verb "have" (have, has)

### Forms in present tense

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I | have | I **have** a phone. |
| you | have | You **have** time. |
| he / she / it | has | She **has** a sister. |
| we | have | We **have** Wi-Fi today. |
| they | have | They **have** books. |

### Main uses

#### 1. Possession (own)
- I **have** a notebook.
- She **has** two phones.

#### 2. Relationships
- I **have** one sister.
- He **has** many friends.

#### 3. Experiences or states
- I **have** time today.
- She **has** a problem.
- We **have** a question.

---

### Negative form: use "do not" or "does not"

| Subject | Negative | Short form | Example |
|---------|----------|------------|---------|
| I / you / we / they | do not have | don't have | I **do not have** Wi-Fi. / I **don't have** Wi-Fi. |
| he / she / it | does not have | doesn't have | She **does not have** time. / She **doesn't have** time. |

**Important:** Do NOT say "I haven't" for simple possession in American English. Say "I **don't have**."

---

### Question form: use "do" or "does"

| Statement | Question | Answer |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| You **have** a phone. | **Do** you **have** a phone? | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| She **has** a sister. | **Does** she **have** a sister? | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |

**Wh- questions:**
- **What** do you have?
- **Who** has a notebook?
- **How many** sisters do you have?

---

### Common "have" expressions

| Expression | Meaning | Example |
|------------|---------|---------|
| have time | be available | Do you **have time** to help? |
| have a question | need to ask | I **have a question**. |
| have a problem | face difficulty | She **has a problem** with her phone. |
| have fun | enjoy | **Have fun** studying! |
| have a break | rest period | Let's **have a break**. |
| have lunch / tea | eat/drink | I **have tea** every morning. |

---

## Be vs Have: Key differences

| Use | Be | Have |
|-----|-----|------|
| Identity | I **am** a student. | *(not used)* |
| Possession | *(not used)* | I **have** a book. |
| Age | I **am** 22. | I **am** 22 years old. (both work) |
| Description | She **is** kind. | *(not used)* |
| State | I **am** tired. | I **have** energy. (noun) |
| Location | I **am** home. | *(not used)* |

**Common confusion:**

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| I have 22 years. | I **am** 22. / I **am** 22 years old. | Age uses "be" |
| I am a phone. | I **have** a phone. | Possession uses "have" |
| She has tired. | She **is** tired. | "Tired" is adjective, use "be" |

> **Dari note:** Dari uses داشتن (*dâštan*) for both possession and some states. English separates these: "I have a book" (possession) vs "I am tired" (state/adjective).

## Practice activities

### Activity 1: Fill in "be"
Complete with am/is/are:
1. I ___ fine.
2. She ___ my sister.
3. They ___ at home.
4. You ___ ready.
5. We ___ students.

**Answers:** 1. am | 2. is | 3. are | 4. are | 5. are

### Activity 2: Fill in "have"
Complete with have/has:
1. I ___ a phone.
2. She ___ two sisters.
3. We ___ time.
4. He ___ a question.
5. They ___ books.

**Answers:** 1. have | 2. has | 3. have | 4. has | 5. have

### Activity 3: Make questions
Turn these into yes/no questions:
1. You are tired. → **Are you tired?**
2. She has a phone. → **Does she have a phone?**
3. They are ready. → **Are they ready?**

### Activity 4: Choose "be" or "have"
1. I ___ happy. (am)
2. She ___ a book. (has)
3. We ___ at home. (are)
4. I ___ time. (have)

---

## Self-check quiz

1. Complete: "I ___ a student." (am / have)
2. Complete: "She ___ a sister." (is / has)
3. Make negative: "I am busy." → "I ___ busy."
4. Make question: "You are ready." → "___ you ready?"
5. Fix this error: "I have tired."

**Answers:** 1. am | 2. has | 3. am not / I'm not | 4. Are | 5. I **am** tired.

## Common mistakes (Dari speakers)

| Mistake | Why wrong | Correct |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| I fine. | Missing "am" | I **am** fine. |
| She have book. | Wrong form with "she" | She **has** a book. |
| I have 20 years. | Wrong verb for age | I **am** 20 (years old). |
| Are you have phone? | Mixed structure | **Do** you **have** a phone? |
| I am have book. | Two verbs | I **have** a book. |
