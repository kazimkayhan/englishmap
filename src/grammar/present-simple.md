# Present Simple

**Learning goal:** Describe habits, facts, and routines

---

## What is present simple?

Present simple is the most common tense in English. You use it for:

1. **Habits and routines** — things you do regularly
2. **Facts** — things that are always true
3. **Permanent situations** — things that don't change often

> **Key pattern:** Subject + base verb (+ **s** for he/she/it)

---

## How to form present simple

### Positive sentences

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I | study | I **study** English every day. |
| you | study | You **study** at home. |
| he / she / it | stud**ies** | She **studies** in the morning. |
| we | study | We **study** together. |
| they | study | They **study** online. |

**Rule:** Add **-s** or **-es** for he/she/it only.

---

### When to add -s, -es, or -ies

| Verb ending | Rule | Examples |
|-------------|------|----------|
| Most verbs | Add **-s** | work → work**s**, eat → eat**s**, read → read**s** |
| -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o | Add **-es** | teach → teach**es**, wash → wash**es**, go → go**es** |
| Consonant + y | Change y to **-ies** | study → stud**ies**, try → tr**ies**, carry → carr**ies** |
| Vowel + y | Add **-s** | play → play**s**, say → say**s**, buy → buy**s** |

**Special verbs:**
- have → **has** (not haves)
- do → **does** (not dos)

> **Dari tip:** Dari verbs don't change based on who is doing the action (من می‌خوانم / او می‌خواند both use "می‌خواند"). English adds -s for he/she/it — practice this daily.

## Uses of present simple

### 1. Habits and routines

Things you do regularly (every day, often, sometimes, never).

- I **wake up** at 6 AM.
- She **drinks** tea every morning.
- We **study** English three times a week.

**Common time expressions:**
- every day / week / month / year
- always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never
- on Mondays, in the morning, at night

---

### 2. Facts and general truths

Things that are always true.

- The sun **rises** in the east.
- Water **boils** at 100°C.
- English **has** 26 letters.
- Kabul **is** in Afghanistan.

---

### 3. Permanent situations

Things that are true for a long time.

- I **live** in Afghanistan.
- She **works** from home.
- They **speak** Dari and Hazaragi.

---

## Negative form: use "do not" / "does not"

| Subject | Negative | Short form | Example |
|---------|----------|------------|---------|
| I / you / we / they | do not + base verb | don't | I **do not study** at night. / I **don't study** at night. |
| he / she / it | does not + base verb | doesn't | She **does not work** on Friday. / She **doesn't work** on Friday. |

**Important rules:**
- Use the **base form** of the verb after don't/doesn't (no -s)
- ✅ She **doesn't study**. (base form)
- ❌ She doesn't studies. (wrong — no -s after doesn't)

**Examples:**
- I **don't drink** coffee.
- He **doesn't have** a computer.
- We **don't go** to school.
- She **doesn't like** noise.

---

## Question form: use "do" / "does"

### Yes/no questions

| Subject | Question form | Answer |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| I / you / we / they | **Do** + subject + base verb? | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. |
| he / she / it | **Does** + subject + base verb? | Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. |

**Examples:**
- **Do** you **study** English? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- **Does** she **work** at home? → Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
- **Do** they **speak** English? → Yes, they do. / No, they don't.

**Again:** Use **base form** after do/does (no -s).

---

### Wh- questions

Put the question word first, then do/does:

| Question word | Structure | Example |
|---------------|-----------|---------|
| What | What + do/does + subject + verb? | **What do** you **study**? |
| Where | Where + do/does + subject + verb? | **Where does** she **live**? |
| When | When + do/does + subject + verb? | **When do** they **work**? |
| Why | Why + do/does + subject + verb? | **Why do** you **learn** English? |
| How | How + do/does + subject + verb? | **How do** you **study**? |
| Who | Who + does + subject + verb? | **Who does** she **help**? |

**Note:** When "who" is the subject, don't use do/does:
- **Who studies** English? → Sara studies English. (no "do")

---

## Time expressions with present simple

| Expression | Position | Example |
|------------|----------|---------|
| every day / week / month | End of sentence | I study **every day**. |
| always, usually, often | Before main verb | I **always** wake up early. |
| sometimes | Before main verb or start | I **sometimes** drink tea. / **Sometimes** I drink tea. |
| never | Before main verb | I **never** skip practice. |
| in the morning / evening | End of sentence | She works **in the morning**. |
| on Monday / Friday | End of sentence | We have class **on Monday**. |

> **Hazaragi tip:** Word order for time expressions may feel different. Practice placing "always/often/sometimes" BEFORE the main verb: "I **always** study," not "I study always."

## Practice activities

### Activity 1: Add -s or -es
Convert these to third person (he/she/it):
1. I work → She ___
2. You teach → He ___
3. We go → It ___
4. They study → She ___
5. I watch → He ___

**Answers:** 1. works | 2. teaches | 3. goes | 4. studies | 5. watches

---

### Activity 2: Make negative sentences
1. I study at night. → I **don't study** at night.
2. She has Wi-Fi. → She **doesn't have** Wi-Fi.
3. They work on Friday. → They **don't work** on Friday.

---

### Activity 3: Make questions
1. You speak English. → **Do** you **speak** English?
2. She lives in Kabul. → **Does** she **live** in Kabul?
3. They study online. → **Do** they **study** online?

---

### Activity 4: Write your routine
Describe your daily routine using 8 present simple sentences.

**Example:**
- I wake up at 6:30.
- I have tea and bread.
- I study English for 30 minutes.
- My sister works from home.
- We eat lunch together.
- I read in the afternoon.
- I don't go out at night.
- I sleep at 10 PM.

---

## Self-check quiz

1. Add the right form: She ___ (study) English every day. *(studies)*
2. Make negative: He works at home. → He ___ at home. *(doesn't work)*
3. Make question: They speak Dari. → ___ they ___ Dari? *(Do / speak)*
4. Fix this error: "She don't like tea."
5. Where do you put "always" in this sentence: "I am tired"?

**Answers:** 1. studies | 2. doesn't work | 3. Do / speak | 4. She **doesn't** like tea. | 5. I **am always** tired. (after "be")

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why wrong | Correct |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| She study English. | Missing -s | She **studies** English. |
| He don't work. | Wrong form | He **doesn't** work. |
| Does she works? | Extra -s after "does" | **Does** she **work**? |
| I always am tired. | Wrong position | I **am always** tired. |
| Do you can help me? | Mixed with modal | **Can** you help me? |

---

## Present simple vs present continuous

Many learners confuse these two. Here's the difference:

| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Habits, routines, facts | Happening **right now** |
| I **study** every day. | I **am studying** right now. |
| She **works** at home. | She **is working** at the moment. |

**If it happens regularly → present simple**  
**If it's happening at this exact moment → present continuous (covered in [Intermediate Growth](../10-intermediate-growth.md))**
