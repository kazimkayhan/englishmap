# Assessments and Progress Checkpoints

**All stages** · Try first · Keys in [answer-keys/](answer-keys/)

---

## Learning goals

1. Find your **starting level** (placement)
2. Check progress at **4 checkpoints**
3. Score **speaking** and **writing** with rubrics
4. Know when to **move up** a stage
5. Use results to **adjust** your study plan

---

## Why assessment matters

Tests are not punishment. They are a **mirror** — they show what to study next.

> *A low score today is data, not destiny.*

## Placement quiz (25 questions)

**Instructions:** 45 minutes max. No dictionary. No answer keys until finished.

Check answers: **[answer-keys/14-placement-quiz-KEYS.md](answer-keys/14-placement-quiz-KEYS.md)**

### Part A — Foundations (1–8)

1. Write the English alphabet in order (or recite and write).
2. What is 14 in English words?
3. Complete: She ___ a student. (am / is / are)
4. Complete: They ___ English every day. (study / studies)
5. Negative: He likes tea. → He ___ ___ tea.
6. Question: You live in Kabul. → ___ you live in Kabul?
7. Choose: There ___ two books. (is / are)
8. Translate idea to English (write one sentence): *I thank you.*

### Part B — Grammar & vocab (9–16)

9. Past of *go*: ___
10. Complete: I ___ studying when she called. (was / were / am)
11. Future: I ___ visit tomorrow. (will / am will / wills)
12. Modal: You ___ rest if you are sick. (should / must both OK — choose one)
13. Opposite of *hot*: ___
14. Write one sentence with *because*.
15. Article: I need ___ pen. (a / an / the)
16. Write 5 words on the topic *study*.

### Part C — Intermediate (17–22)

17. Past participle used with *have*: I have ___ (finish) → ___
18. Second conditional: If I ___ (have) more time, I ___ (study) more.
19. Relative clause: The woman ___ teaches online is kind. (who / which)
20. Formal: Choose better: *I want help* OR *I would like assistance*
21. Write 2 sentences: past + future about your English learning.
22. Connector: Complete with one word: I was tired, ___ I continued.

### Part D — Advanced (23–25)

23. Passive: English ___ (teach) in many countries.
24. Hedge: Rewrite softer: *English is easy.* → ___
25. Write 3–4 sentences: opinion on why education matters for women.

## Checkpoint 1 — End of beginner (Month 3)

**When:** After [study-plans/12-week-beginner.md](study-plans/12-week-beginner.md)

**Sections:**

1. Write 8 present tense sentences about your routine.
2. Listen to 1-min slow audio — write 3 sentences you understood.
3. Record 60-sec introduction.
4. Read short text in [08-reading.md](08-reading.md) — answer 3 questions.
5. Vocabulary: write 20 words from memory on topic *home*.

**Pass guide:** Complete 4/5 sections with understandable English.

**Drills:** [exercises/14-assessment-drills.md](exercises/14-assessment-drills.md)  
**Keys:** [answer-keys/14-assessment-drills-KEYS.md](answer-keys/14-assessment-drills-KEYS.md)

## Checkpoint 2 — Mid-intermediate (Month 6)

1. Write a 120-word paragraph: *A challenge I overcame* (past tense).
2. Use 5 linking words (because, however, first, then, finally).
3. 2-min recorded story.
4. Dictation: 5 sentences from listening module.
5. Answer: What will you do next month for English? (future forms)

**Pass guide:** Paragraph mostly clear; past tense mostly correct.

---

## Checkpoint 3 — Pre-advanced (Month 12)

1. Write formal email (100+ words) — request information about a course.
2. One second conditional sentence about your life.
3. 3-min presentation recording — outline in [11-advanced-skills.md](11-advanced-skills.md).
4. Read news article — summarize in 5 sentences.
5. Comprehension quiz in exercises file.

---

## Checkpoint 4 — Advanced review (Month 18–24)

1. Argument paragraph (150 words) with claim + evidence + counter.
2. Passive voice: 3 sentences about education.
3. Informal vs formal: rewrite one casual message formally.
4. 5-min presentation on *My vision for my future*.
5. Self-score all rubrics below — honest ratings.

## Speaking rubric (self-score 1–5)

| Criteria | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|----------|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Hard to understand | Mostly clear | Clear, easy |
| Fluency | Long pauses | Some pauses | Smooth for level |
| Grammar | Many errors | Some errors | Few errors for level |
| Vocabulary | Very basic | Adequate | Rich for topic |
| Confidence | Whisper / stop | Uneven | Steady voice |

**Goal:** Average **3+** at beginner checkpoint; **4+** at advanced.

---

## Writing rubric (self-score 1–5)

| Criteria | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|----------|---|---|---|
| Task completion | Off topic | Mostly on topic | Fully on topic |
| Organization | No clear order | Some structure | Clear paragraphs |
| Grammar | Blocks meaning | Some mistakes | Minor mistakes |
| Vocabulary | Repetitive | Some variety | Good variety |
| Mechanics | No caps/periods | Some errors | Few errors |

---

## Listening rubric (self-score 1–5)

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Only isolated words |
| 2 | Main idea with repetition |
| 3 | Main idea first listen, slow audio |
| 4 | Details with one replay, normal speed short |
| 5 | Podcast 10 min with notes |

## When to move up a stage

| From | To | Evidence |
|------|-----|----------|
| Stage 0 | 1 | Alphabet + 100 words + intro |
| Stage 1 | 2 | Checkpoint 1 pass + 300 words |
| Stage 2 | 3 | Checkpoint 2 pass + past/future stable |
| Stage 3 | 4 | Checkpoint 3 pass + formal writing |
| Stage 4 | maintain | Media, work, teaching others |

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Cheating on placement | Start too hard → frustration |
| Skipping speaking/writing in checkpoints | Do all sections |
| One perfect score obsession | Track trend over months |
| Never retesting | Retest after 3 months |
