# Listening Skills

**Stage 1 → Stage 4** · 10–15 min daily recommended

---

## Learning goals

1. Understand **slow, clear** English (Stage 1)
2. Move to **natural speed** short clips (Stage 2+)
3. Use **dictation** to train your ear
4. **Wean off subtitles** gradually
5. Keep learning when **internet fails** (downloaded audio)

---

## Why this skill matters

Real English is heard before it is read. Listening trains your brain for **speed, rhythm, and real grammar** — not only textbook English.

> *If you understand 60% of a clip, it is the right level. Not 95% — too easy. Not 20% — too hard.*

---

## Level focus

**Stage 1:** slow · **Stage 2:** normal short · **Stage 3+:** podcasts, lectures

---

## Step-by-step lessons

### Lesson 1 — Listening levels (10 min)

| Level | What you hear | Goal |
|-------|---------------|------|
| 1 | Single words, slow | Recognize sounds |
| 2 | Short sentences, slow | Catch subject + verb |
| 3 | 1–2 min dialogue, clear | Main idea |
| 4 | News/podcast clips | Details + opinion |
| 5 | Movies, fast speech | Slang, humor (advanced) |

Move up when you understand **main idea** without pausing every line.

---

### Lesson 2 — Daily listening routine (15 min)

| Step | Minutes | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| 1 | 2 | Predict topic from title |
| 2 | 5 | Listen once — no text |
| 3 | 5 | Listen again — note 3 words you heard |
| 4 | 3 | Listen with transcript — check |
| 5 | optional | Shadow last sentence (repeat aloud) |

---

### Lesson 3 — Dictation (15 min)

1. Play **one sentence** (slow English)
2. Pause
3. Write what you hear
4. Check transcript
5. Fix spelling and missed words

**Beginner sentence example:**  
*“I study English at home every day.”*

---

### Lesson 4 — Subtitle strategy (10 min)

| Phase | Subtitles |
|-------|-----------|
| Week 1–4 | English subtitles ON |
| Month 2–3 | English subtitles, pause less |
| Month 4+ | Try without subtitles, then check |
| Advanced | No subtitles first |

**Avoid:** Dari subtitles only — your ear will not train.

---

### Lesson 5 — Offline and weak internet (10 min)

- **Download** videos/audio on Wi-Fi (YouTube offline, podcast apps)
- Save **2–3 clips** for the week
- Repeat same clip **3 days** — deep learning beats endless new content
- Partner/sister: one downloads, shares file if safe and allowed

---

### Lesson 6 — Listening for exams and messages (Stage 2+)

- **Scan** for numbers, names, times
- **Signal words:** but (contrast), because (reason), so (result)
- Voice messages: listen twice before replying

---

## Examples

**What to write after listening (Level 3):**

> Topic: studying at home  
> Speaker: woman, American  
> Main idea: she studies 30 minutes daily  
> Words I heard: practice, improve, patient

**Dictation check:**

Audio: *“She wants to learn English for her future.”*  
Your write: *She wants to learn english for her future.*  
Fix: capitalize English; check *wants* ending.

## Self-check questions

1. What is the 60% understanding rule?
2. Name the 5 steps of the daily routine.
3. Why should English subtitles beat Dari-only subtitles?
4. How many times should you reuse a good-level clip?
5. What do signal words *but* and *because* tell you?

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Only watching, not listening | Close screen sometimes — audio only |
| Always max speed | Start slow; speed up gradually |
| New clip every day | Repeat one clip 3 days |
| Giving up at 30% understanding | Choose easier level |
