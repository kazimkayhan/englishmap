# First 100 Words

**Learning goal:** Use 100 essential words in simple sentences

---

## Why these words matter

These 100 words appear in almost every English conversation. Learn them well, and you can understand basic messages, introduce yourself, and start building sentences.

> **Success metric:** By week 2, you should recognize 80+ of these words without translating in your head.

---

## How to learn each word

For every new word, write down:

1. **The English word** — spell it correctly
2. **Simple meaning** — one or two words in Dari if needed, but try English explanation first
3. **One example sentence** — use it in YOUR life
4. **Say it aloud** — at least twice

**Do NOT just read lists silently.** Speak every word. Use it the same day.

## Group 1: Personal pronouns & be (10 words)

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| I | myself | **I** study English. |
| you | the person I'm talking to | **You** are my friend. |
| he | a man/boy | **He** is my brother. |
| she | a woman/girl | **She** works at home. |
| we | me and others | **We** learn together. |
| they | other people | **They** speak English. |
| am | be (with I) | I **am** tired. |
| is | be (with he/she/it) | She **is** kind. |
| are | be (with you/we/they) | You **are** ready. |
| it | thing or idea | **It** is a good book. |

> **Dari tip:** In Dari you often drop the "to be" verb (هستم / است) in present tense. In English, you MUST include am/is/are: "I am fine" not "I fine."

## Group 10: Connecting words (5 words)

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| or | choice between | Tea **or** water? |
| not | negative | I am **not** ready. |
| can | able to | I **can** read this. |

## Practice activities

### Activity 1: Self-introduction
Write 5 sentences about yourself using words from Groups 1, 2, 3, and 7.

**Example:**
- I am Sara.
- I live in Kabul.
- I study English at home.
- I have one sister.
- I want to learn.

### Activity 2: Daily routine
Describe your morning using 10 words from the list.

### Activity 3: Flashcards
- Write each word on one line in your notes app
- Write the meaning on the next line
- Review 10 words before bed

### Activity 4: Speaking practice
Read each group aloud twice. Record yourself.

## Self-check quiz

1. Complete: "I ___ tired." (am / is / are)
2. What is the question word for "place"? (where / when / who)
3. Make a sentence with: home, study, I
4. Translate to English: "لطفاً به من کمک کن" (Use "please" and "help")
5. Which word means "not old"? (new / small / happy)

**Answers:** 1. am | 2. where | 3. "I study at home." | 4. "Please help me." | 5. new

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it's wrong | Correct |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| I happy | Missing "am" | I **am** happy |
| Where you live? | Missing "do" | Where **do** you live? |
| She have book | Wrong form with "she" | She **has** a book |
| I no understand | Wrong negative | I do **not** understand |
