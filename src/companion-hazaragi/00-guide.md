# راهنمای هزارگی — Hazaragi Guide Introduction

**English for Hazara Speakers — Learning American English from a Hazaragi Perspective**

---

## Welcome — خوش آمدید

This is the **first comprehensive Hazaragi companion guide** for learning English. Hazaragi is a rich, valid language variety spoken by millions of Hazara people, primarily in Afghanistan (Hazarajat region) and diaspora communities worldwide.

**This guide recognizes:**
- Hazaragi is **not** "broken Dari" — it's a distinct dialect with its own phonology, vocabulary, and grammar
- Hazaragi speakers face **unique pronunciation challenges** when learning English
- Common Hazaragi words and expressions deserve **direct translation**, not only Dari bridges
- Accent notes: "how we say it" in Hazaragi vs Standard Dari vs English

---

## Who This Guide Is For

- **Hazara women and girls** learning English at home
- Speakers of **Hazaragi** (هزارگی) as first or heritage language
- Learners who think in Hazaragi but read Dari script
- Anyone who says *"I speak Hazaragi differently than Dari speakers"*

**You are seen. Your language is valid. This guide is for you.**

---

## Hazaragi vs Dari vs English

| Feature | Hazaragi | Standard Dari | English |
|---------|----------|---------------|---------|
| **Vowels** | Simplified (e/i often merge) | 6-7 vowels | 12-15 vowel sounds |
| **Consonants** | Some unique (like *ř*) | Persian set | New sounds: *th, w, v* |
| **Word Order** | SOV (flexible) | SOV (flexible) | SVO (strict) |
| **Articles** | None | None | *a, an, the* required |
| **Common Words** | *mardim* (people), *aftow* (sun) | *mardom*, *āftāb* | *people*, *sun* |

**Key insight:** Hazaragi and Dari speakers both learn English as a foreign language, but with **different starting sounds and vocabulary**. This guide addresses Hazaragi-specific challenges.

## Spelling & Transliteration Note

**Hazaragi has no standardized orthography.** Different writers spell words differently. This guide uses:

- **Perso-Arabic script** where possible (هزارگی)
- **Modified Dari spelling** with pronunciation notes
- **Latin transliteration** for pronunciation clarity

Examples:
- مردم → *mardim* (Hazaragi) vs *mardom* (Dari) = "people"
- آفتاب → *aftow* (Hazaragi) vs *āftāb* (Dari) = "sun"
- دریا → *daryow* (Hazaragi) vs *daryā* (Dari) = "sea/river"

**If you spell it differently in your community, that's valid too.** Focus on the sound and meaning.

## A Note on Language Pride

**Hazaragi is not inferior to Dari or English.**

- Speaking Hazaragi at home while learning English is **normal and good**
- Your accent in English will be influenced by Hazaragi — **that's okay**
- Millions of native English speakers have accents too
- Clear communication > perfect accent

**Goals:**
1. Be understood in English (priority)
2. Keep Hazaragi strong (identity)
3. Use Dari when needed (written standard)
4. Code-switch confidently (superpower)

You are **multilingual** — that's a strength, not a barrier.

## Common Questions

### Q: Should I learn Dari first, then English?
**A:** No. You can learn English directly from Hazaragi. This guide helps you do that.

### Q: Will my Hazaragi accent hurt my English?
**A:** Every learner has an accent. Focus on clear pronunciation of key sounds (*th, w, v*). Hazaragi accent ≠ bad English.

### Q: Can I use Dari materials if I speak Hazaragi?
**A:** Yes! Use Dari companion for grammar, Hazaragi companion for pronunciation and daily words. Mix freely.

### Q: Why isn't there more Hazaragi content online?
**A:** Hazaragi is primarily oral and not standardized in writing. This guide is a step toward more resources. **You can help by learning and teaching others.**
