# Common Hazaragi-English Vocabulary

**Direct translations for everyday Hazaragi words**

---

## How to Use This List

- **Hazaragi word** → English equivalent
- **Dari comparison** (when different)
- **Pronunciation** in Latin script
- **Example sentences** in both languages

**Note:** Hazaragi has regional variations. If your family uses a different word, that's valid too!

---

## People & Family — مردم و خانواده

| Hazaragi | Dari | English | Notes |
|----------|------|---------|-------|
| **مردیم** *mardim* | مردم *mardom* | people | Hazaragi *i* instead of Dari *o* |
| **زن** *zan* | زن *zan* | woman | Same |
| **مرد** *mard* | مرد *mard* | man | Same |
| **دختر** *dokhtar* | دختر *dokhtar* | girl, daughter | Same |
| **پسر** *pisar* | پسر *pesar* | boy, son | Same |
| **مادر / مار** *mādar / mār* | مادر *mādar* | mother | Hazaragi often shortens to *mār* |
| **پدر / په** *padar / pa* | پدر *pedar* | father | Hazaragi often shortens to *pa* or *bāwa* |
| **خوار / خواهر** *khwār* | خواهر *khwāhar* | sister | Hazaragi simplifies |
| **برار / برادر** *barār* | برادر *barādar* | brother | Hazaragi drops middle *d* |
| **کاکه** *kāka* | کاکا *kākā* | uncle (paternal) | Slight vowel difference |
| **مامه** *māma* | ماما *māmā* | uncle (maternal) | Same |

### Example Sentences

**Hazaragi:**
> *Mardim zindagí besyār sakht darand.*

**English:**
> "People have a very difficult life."

**Hazaragi:**
> *Mār-am dar khor ast.*

**English:**
> "My mother is at home."

## Common Verbs — افعال رایج

| Hazaragi Infinitive | Dari | English | Notes |
|---------------------|------|---------|-------|
| **بیریم** *berem* | بروم *berawam* / *mirawam* | to go (I go) | Hazaragi *-e-* vowel |
| **بکونیم** *bekonim* | بکنم *bokonam* | to do (I do) | Vowel difference |
| **بگیم** *begēm* | بگویم *begōyam* | to say (I say) | Simplified |
| **بُوینیم** *bewīnim* | ببینم *bebinam* | to see (I see) | *w* for *b* in some Hazaragi |
| **بخورم** *bekhōram* | بخورم *bekhōram* | to eat (I eat) | Same |
| **بنوشم** *benōsham* | بنوشم *benōsham* | to drink (I drink) | Same |
| **بخوم** *bekhom* | بخوابم *bekhābam* | to sleep (I sleep) | Simplified |
| **بیریم** *beyāram* | بیاورم *beyāwaram* | to bring (I bring) | Simplified |

**Note:** Hazaragi verbs often drop syllables or simplify compared to Dari. This is normal and systematic.

### Example Sentences

**Hazaragi:**
> *Har rōz engelīsī mī-khōnam.*

**English:**
> "I read/study English every day."

(Note: *mī-khōnam* = present continuous "I am reading/studying")

## Hazaragi-English Sentence Patterns

### Present Tense

**Hazaragi:**
> *Man engelīsī mī-khōnam.*

**Word-for-word:**
> "I English [present marker]-read."

**English:**
> "I study/read English."

### Past Tense

**Hazaragi:**
> *Man dērōz ketow khāndom.*

**Word-for-word:**
> "I yesterday book read-[past]."

**English:**
> "I read a book yesterday."

### Future (using *khāham* / "I will")

**Hazaragi:**
> *Man fardā be maktab khāham raft.*

**Word-for-word:**
> "I tomorrow to school will go-[past participle]."

**English:**
> "I will go to school tomorrow."

## Study Tips: Hazaragi → English

1. **Don't translate word-for-word**
   - Hazaragi: SOV (Subject-Object-Verb)
   - English: SVO (Subject-Verb-Object)
   - Example: *"I book read"* → *"I read a book"*

2. **Add articles** (*a, an, the*)
   - Hazaragi has no articles
   - English requires them: *"I have book"* → *"I have **a** book"*

3. **English needs pronouns as subjects**
   - Hazaragi: *Mī-khōnam* (verb alone, "I read/am reading")
   - English: *I read* (subject required)

4. **Hazaragi plurals are flexible; English plurals are strict**
   - Hazaragi: *mardim* can mean "people" (already plural)
   - English: *man* → *men*, *woman* → *women* (irregular)

## Regional Variations Note

Hazaragi varies across:
- **Hazarajat (Bamyan, Daikundi, Ghazni, etc.)** — Central Afghan Hazaragi
- **Quetta (Pakistan)** — Quetta Hazaragi has Urdu/Pashto influence
- **Iran** — Iranian Hazaragi has more Persian influence
- **Diaspora** — Mixed features

**This guide uses Central Afghan Hazaragi as the baseline.** If your words differ, they're still valid!

## Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Translate to English

1. *Man dar kor-am engelīsī mī-khōnam.*
2. *Mār-am ketow mī-khōnad.*
3. *Aftow besyār garm ast.*

**Answers:**
1. I study English at home.
2. My mother reads a book.
3. The sun is very hot.

### Exercise 2: Translate to Hazaragi (try first, then check)

1. I drink water every day.
2. My brother goes to school.
3. She is tired.

**Suggested Hazaragi:**
1. *Man har rōz āw mī-nōsham.*
2. *Barār-am be maktab mī-berem.*
3. *Ow khasta ast.* (ow = she/he)
