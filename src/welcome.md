## Who This Is For

This comprehensive English learning guide was created especially for **young women and girls in Afghanistan** (ages 15-25) whose education has been interrupted or restricted. If you are studying at home, often in difficult circumstances, with limited resources — **this book is for you**.

## Your Learning Journey

This book will take you from **zero English** to **confident, professional English** (A0 → C1-C2).

| Starting Point | Time Commitment | Outcome |
|---------------|-----------------|---------|
| No English at all | ~30-45 min/day | Strong English in 18-24 months |
| Some basic words | 3-5 days/week | Steady progress, longer timeline |
| Interrupted often | Return anytime | The book waits for you |

### Five Clear Stages

```
Stage 0: Pre-beginner     (Weeks 1-2)      → Alphabet, hello, 50 words
Stage 1: Beginner A1-A2   (Months 1-3)     → Daily life, present tense  
Stage 2: Intermediate B1  (Months 4-8)     → Stories, opinions, paragraphs
Stage 3: Upper B2-C1      (Months 9-14)    → Complex topics, formal writing
Stage 4: Advanced C1-C2   (Months 15-24+)  → Professional, nuanced English
```

## Special Features for Dari & Hazaragi Speakers

### Dari Support (دری)
Every major chapter has a Dari companion page explaining:
- Key concepts in your first language
- Common translation challenges  
- Study tips specific to Dari speakers
- Grammar comparisons (Dari vs English)

### Hazaragi Support (هزارگی)
This guide includes the **first comprehensive Hazaragi learning notes** for English:
- Pronunciation differences between Hazaragi, Dari, and English
- Common Hazaragi vocabulary with English equivalents
- Accent notes: "how we say it" in Hazaragi
- Recognition that Hazaragi is a rich, valid language variety

## How to Use This Book

### If You're Brand New to English
1. Start with [Quick Start](./quick-start.md) (15 minutes)
2. Read [Safety & Privacy Guide](./safety-privacy.md)
3. Begin [Beginner Foundations](./02-beginner-foundations.md)
4. Follow the [12-Week Beginner Plan](./plans/12-week.md)

### If You Have Some English Already
1. Take the [Placement Quiz](./placement-quiz.md)
2. Start at your recommended level
3. Choose a study plan that fits your schedule
4. Use the Dari/Hazaragi companions when stuck

### Daily Study Rhythm (30-45 minutes)
```
Day 1: Vocabulary + pronunciation (15+10 min)
Day 2: Grammar + writing (15+10 min)
Day 3: Listening + dictation (20 min)
Day 4: Reading + new words (25 min)
Day 5: Speaking practice (record yourself, 20 min)
Day 6: Review weak areas (30 min)
Day 7: Light review or rest
```

## Safety & Privacy First

### Digital Safety
- Use strong passwords on all learning accounts
- Keep voice recordings in private folders
- Be cautious with personal information online
- Prefer well-known, free apps (see [Resources](./15-resources.md))

### Study Safety
- Choose a private, safe space to study
- Use headphones for listening practice
- You don't owe anyone an explanation for learning
- It's okay to keep your studies private

### Emotional Safety
- Compare yourself to your past self, not to others
- Breaks are normal — restart without shame or guilt
- Missing days doesn't mean failure
- Small progress counts
- Your worth is not determined by your fluency
