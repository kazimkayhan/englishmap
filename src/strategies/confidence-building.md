# Overcoming Shame and Building Confidence

**Learning goal:** Study without shame after breaks or mistakes

---

## Common shame sources

1. "I stopped for [time period]"
2. "My accent is bad"
3. "I make so many mistakes"
4. "Others are better/faster"
5. "I should be fluent by now"

---

## Truth reframes

| Shame thought | Truth |
|---------------|-------|
| I stopped for 3 weeks | I am restarting today. That is courage. |
| My accent is bad | My accent shows I'm multilingual. |
| I make mistakes | Mistakes mean I'm practicing. |
| Others are faster | I compare only to past me. |
| Should be fluent | Language takes years. I'm progressing. |

---

## Daily affirmations

Say aloud (or in your heart):
- I am a capable learner
- Small progress is still progress
- My voice in English is valid
- I restart as many times as needed
- Education is my right

---

## When criticism comes

**External:** If someone mocks your accent/level:
- Their opinion doesn't define your worth
- Leave the conversation if needed
- Find supportive practice partners

**Internal:** If you criticize yourself:
- Would you say this to a friend learning Dari? No? Then don't say it to yourself.
- Note ONE thing done well today
- Speak kindly: "I'm learning" not "I'm terrible"

---

## Shame recovery script

When shame strikes:

1. **Acknowledge:** "I feel ashamed because..."
2. **Truth:** "But the truth is, learning takes time."
3. **Action:** "Today I will do [small step]."
4. **Kindness:** "I am doing my best."
