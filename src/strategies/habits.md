# Building Habits

**Learning goal:** Create sustainable English learning routines

---

## The habit loop

1. **Cue:** Time/place triggers study
2. **Routine:** The study session itself
3. **Reward:** Check mark, progress feeling

---

## Start small

Don't begin with 2 hours daily. Begin with:
- **Week 1:** 10 minutes daily (build consistency)
- **Week 2-4:** 15-20 minutes (comfortable level)
- **Month 2+:** 30-45 minutes (sustainable pace)

---

## Habit stacking

Attach English to existing habit:
- After breakfast → 10 words
- While tea cools → listen to clip
- Before bed → write 3 sentences

---

## Track visually

- Calendar with X marks
- Tracker in [Progress Tracker](../16-tracker.md)
- Chain of days completed

**Don't break the chain!**

---

## When habits break

Life interrupts. To restart:
1. Do 5 minutes today (not zero)
2. Return to normal schedule tomorrow
3. No punishment, just continue
