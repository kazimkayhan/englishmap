# Maintaining Motivation

**Learning goal:** Stay motivated through challenges

---

## Intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation

**Extrinsic** (external): job, certificate, approval  
**Intrinsic** (internal): curiosity, growth, meaning

Both are valid. Intrinsic lasts longer.

---

## Your "why"

Write down (keep private):
- **Why** I learn English: [your reasons]
- **What** it will help me do: [specific goals]
- **How** I will feel when fluent: [emotions]

Read this monthly.

---

## Motivation strategies

### When bored
- Change skill (grammar → listening)
- Try new material (podcast, video)
- Study favorite topic

### When overwhelmed
- Drop to 15-minute minimum
- Review easy material (builds confidence)
- Skip one skill temporarily

### When lonely
- Join online community (safe)
- Study with sister/friend
- Record voice messages to yourself

---

## Small wins matter

Celebrate:
- Spoke 1 sentence without stopping
- Understood one full clip
- Studied 7 days straight
- Used new word in real life
