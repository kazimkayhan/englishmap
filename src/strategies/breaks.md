# Dealing with Breaks

**Learning goal:** Restart learning after interruptions

---

## Breaks are normal

Illness, family needs, stress, no internet — breaks happen.

**Key truth:** The break doesn't define you. The restart does.

---

## After 1-7 days off

1. Review last 10 words learned
2. Do today's minimum (15 min)
3. Resume full schedule tomorrow

**Do NOT try to "catch up" with double sessions.**

---

## After 1-4 weeks off

1. Review one easy module (builds confidence)
2. Start at 15 min/day for 3 days
3. Return to 30-45 min after that
4. May need to drop back one level temporarily

---

## After 1+ months off

1. Retake placement quiz
2. Review fundamentals (alphabet, common words if needed)
3. Restart 12-week plan from beginning
4. Will progress faster second time (not starting from zero)

---

## Overcoming shame

"I stopped" = human  
"I restarted" = brave

No apologies needed to yourself. Just open the book and begin.
