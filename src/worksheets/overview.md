# Worksheet Overview

**Learning goal:** Use worksheets for focused practice

---

## Available worksheets

Located in `assets/worksheets/`:
- Alphabet practice
- Grammar exercises (be/have, present tense)
- Vocabulary logs
- Phonics & blends
- Listening dictation templates
- Writing journal templates
- Progress review sheets

---

## How to use worksheets

1. **Print or copy:** Write in notebook or type on phone
2. **Complete independently:** No looking at answers first
3. **Self-check:** Use answer keys after completion
4. **Repeat:** Redo worksheets monthly to see progress

---

## Digital alternative

Can't print? Create your own version in notes app following the template structure.
