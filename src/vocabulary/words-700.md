# High-Frequency Words (301-700)

**Learning goal:** Expand vocabulary to 700 most common words

---

## Why words 301-700 matter

Once you know the first 300 words, these next 400 words:
- Increase your comprehension to **75-80%** of written English
- Let you express more complex ideas
- Help you understand longer conversations
- Enable you to read simple articles and stories

> **Study pace:** Continue with 10-15 words per day. At this rate, you'll finish in about 4 weeks.

---

## How this level is different

Words 1-300 are mostly:
- Simple, everyday words
- Single-syllable or two-syllable words
- Basic nouns, verbs, adjectives

Words 301-700 include:
- More specific nouns (government, education, health)
- Action verbs (decide, explain, develop)
- Abstract concepts (idea, reason, result)
- More adjectives and adverbs

**You'll need to practice these words in sentences more actively.**

## Suggested learning groups (4-week plan)

### Week 1: Words 301-400 (civic & social words)
Focus: government, society, community, relationships

**Sample words:** government, president, law, rule, country, state, city, town, community, society, public, private, group, team, member, leader, follow, join, support, vote, agree, decide, choose, change, improve, develop, grow, increase, build, create, organize

**Practice:** Write about your community or country using 15 words from this group.

---

### Week 2: Words 401-500 (education & work)
Focus: learning, jobs, actions, progress

**Sample words:** education, school, university, course, lesson, teach, learn, study, practice, test, exam, pass, fail, grade, knowledge, skill, ability, improve, progress, develop, job, work, career, business, office, manager, worker, employee, company, service, product, sell, buy, pay, cost, price, money, dollar, save, spend, earn

**Practice:** Describe your study routine or a job using 15 words.

---

### Week 3: Words 501-600 (description & comparison)
Focus: adjectives, adverbs, comparisons

**Sample words:** different, similar, same, equal, various, several, many, much, few, little, more, most, less, least, better, best, worse, worst, bigger, smaller, older, newer, faster, slower, stronger, weaker, higher, lower, longer, shorter, earlier, later, easier, harder, simpler, clearer, important, necessary, possible, impossible, difficult, easy, hard, soft, heavy, light, full, empty, clean, dirty

**Practice:** Compare two things (phone vs computer, morning vs night, etc.)

---

### Week 4: Words 601-700 (time, reason, result)
Focus: connecting ideas, explaining cause and effect

**Sample words:** because, so, therefore, since, although, however, yet, still, already, just, only, even, almost, quite, rather, perhaps, maybe, probably, certainly, surely, truly, really, actually, finally, eventually, suddenly, recently, ago, before, after, during, while, until, since, for, reason, cause, result, effect, happen, occur, lead, follow, continue, stop, begin, end, finish, complete

**Practice:** Write 10 sentences explaining why you do things ("I study because..." / "I practice so that...")

## Study methods for this level

### Method 1: Sentence mining
- Read a simple English article
- Find 5 words from the 301-700 list
- Write your own sentences with those words

### Method 2: Thematic paragraphs
Write one paragraph per theme:
- **Education:** Use 10 school/study words
- **Family & society:** Use 10 relationship words
- **Goals:** Use 10 future/action words

### Method 3: Synonym practice
Many words 301-700 are synonyms of words 1-300:
- big → large, huge, enormous
- small → little, tiny
- good → nice, fine, excellent
- bad → poor, terrible, awful

Learn word families together.

### Method 4: Collocations (words that go together)
- **make** a decision
- **take** a test
- **do** homework
- **give** advice
- **pay** attention
- **break** a rule

Learn verbs with their common noun partners.

## Integration with grammar

As you learn words 301-700, practice advanced patterns:

- **Past tense:** "Yesterday, I **decided** to study more."
- **Future:** "I **will explain** it tomorrow."
- **Modals:** "You **should improve** your skills."
- **Comparatives:** "This method is **more effective**."
- **Conditionals:** "**If** you **practice**, you **will improve**."

## Self-check quiz (after each 100 words)

1. Can you write 20 words from this group from memory?
2. Can you use 10 of them in sentences without looking?
3. Can you explain 5 words in simple English (without Dari)?
4. Can you recognize them when reading?

If yes to all → move to next group.  
If no → review for 3 more days.

---

## Common challenges

### Challenge 1: Similar words
Many words sound similar or have close meanings:
- affect / effect
- accept / except
- advice / advise

**Solution:** Learn one at a time with clear examples.

### Challenge 2: Abstract words
Words like "concept," "principle," "theory" are harder because they're abstract.

**Solution:** Connect them to concrete examples from your life.

### Challenge 3: Longer words
Words like "government" (3 syllables) or "education" (4 syllables) are harder to remember.

**Solution:** Break them into syllables: gov-ern-ment, ed-u-ca-tion. Practice stress.

## Daily practice routine (15 minutes)

**Morning (5 min):** Review yesterday's 10-15 words  
**Midday (5 min):** Learn 10-15 new words  
**Evening (5 min):** Write 3 sentences using new words + say them aloud
