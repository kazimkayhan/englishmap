# Past Tenses

**Learning goal:** Tell stories and describe past experiences

---

## Past Simple

**Regular verbs:** Add -ed
- walk → walked
- study → studied
- play → played

**Irregular verbs (learn weekly):**
- go → went
- see → saw
- have → had
- make → made
- take → took

**Use:** Completed actions in the past

*Yesterday I studied for two hours. Last week I went to the market.*

---

## Past Continuous

**Was/were + verb-ing**

**Use:** Actions in progress at specific past time

*I was studying when my sister called.*

---

## Past Simple vs Past Continuous

- **Simple:** I studied English yesterday. (finished action)
- **Continuous:** I was studying when you arrived. (in progress)

---

## Practice

1. Write 10 sentences about last week using past simple
2. Write 5 sentences using past continuous
3. Combine: Write a story using both
