# Future Forms

**Learning goal:** Express plans, predictions, and promises

---

## Three main future forms

### 1. Will

**Use:** Decisions made now, promises, predictions

- I will study tomorrow.
- It will rain later.
- I will help you.

### 2. Going to

**Use:** Plans already decided, predictions with evidence

- I am going to finish this module today. (plan)
- Look at those clouds — it's going to rain. (evidence)

### 3. Present Continuous for future

**Use:** Fixed arrangements

- I am meeting my sister tonight to practice English.
- We are having class at 6 PM.

---

## Choosing the right form

| Situation | Form | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|
| Sudden decision | will | I'll help you right now! |
| Pre-planned | going to | I'm going to study tonight. |
| Scheduled event | present continuous | Class is starting at 9 AM. |
