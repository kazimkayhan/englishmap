# Building Fluency

**Learning goal:** Speak and write smoothly without long pauses

---

## What is fluency?

Fluency = **smooth communication** even with some mistakes

**Not:** Perfect grammar at slow speed  
**Yes:** Clear communication at natural pace

---

## Fluency-building techniques

### 1. Timed writing (10 min)
Write continuously for 10 minutes without stopping. Don't edit, just write.

### 2. 2-minute talks
Speak about one topic for 2 minutes without stopping. Record yourself.

### 3. Shadowing
Repeat after native speakers to match rhythm and flow.

### 4. Reduce self-correction
Let small mistakes go while speaking. Fix them later in writing.

### 5. Use chunks
Learn phrases as units: "as a matter of fact," "to be honest"

---

## Fluency vs accuracy

- **Beginner:** Focus on accuracy (correct sentences)
- **Intermediate:** Balance both
- **Advanced:** Fluency with monitoring

---

## Weekly fluency challenges

- **Week 1:** Write 150 words in 10 minutes daily
- **Week 2:** Speak for 2 minutes on 5 different topics
- **Week 3:** Shadow 5 minutes of audio daily
- **Week 4:** Have one 10-minute conversation (with self, partner, or recording)
