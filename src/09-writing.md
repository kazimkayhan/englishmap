# Writing Skills

**Stage 1 → Stage 4** · 10–15 min daily

---

## Learning goals

1. Write **correct simple sentences** (SVO order)
2. Compose **chat-length** and **email-length** messages
3. Build **paragraphs** with topic sentence + details
4. Use **punctuation** and capitalization
5. Edit your own work with a **simple checklist**

---

## Why this skill matters

Writing makes thinking clear. It helps you apply for courses, send professional messages, and express yourself when speaking is not possible.

> *Write messy first. Edit second. Courage first, perfection later.*

---

## Level focus

**Stage 1:** sentences · **Stage 2:** paragraphs · **Stage 3+:** formal writing

---

## Step-by-step lessons

### Lesson 1 — Sentence skeleton (15 min)

**Subject + Verb + Object** (American order)

| Part | Example |
|------|---------|
| Subject | I |
| Verb | study |
| Object | English |

- I study English. ✓  
- Study I English. ✗

> **Dari note:** Dari allows flexible order. English is stricter — subject usually **first**.

---

### Lesson 2 — Capitalization and punctuation (15 min)

**Capitalize:**

- First word of sentence
- I
- Names, places: Fatima, Afghanistan, Monday
- Language: English

**Punctuation:**

| Mark | Use |
|------|-----|
| . | end of statement |
| ? | question |
| ! | strong feeling (use rarely) |
| , | short pause in list or clause |

**Example:**

> Hello, my name is Laila. I live in Afghanistan. Do you study English?

---

### Lesson 3 — Chat writing (10 min)

Short, clear, polite:

> Good morning! I am fine today. I studied English for 30 minutes. How are you?  

Avoid: all caps (looks angry), too many !!! 

---

### Lesson 4 — Daily journal (15 min)

**Template (5 sentences):**

1. Today I…  
2. I felt…  
3. I learned…  
4. Tomorrow I will…  
5. I am grateful for…

Optional: journal helps emotions — keep private.

---

### Lesson 5 — Paragraph structure (20 min)

**One paragraph = 5–8 sentences**

1. **Topic sentence** — main idea  
2. **Detail** — example or reason  
3. **Detail** — second example  
4. **Closing** — summary or feeling

**Example topic:** Why I study English

> I study English because it gives me independence. I can take online courses and read global news. I practice thirty minutes every day at home on my phone. Some days are hard, but I continue. English is a door to my future, and I choose to open it.

---

### Lesson 6 — Linking words (15 min)

| Type | Words |
|------|-------|
| Add | and, also, moreover |
| Contrast | but, however, although |
| Reason | because, since |
| Order | first, then, finally |
| Example | for example, such as |

---

### Lesson 7 — Self-editing checklist (10 min)

Before you finish:

- [ ] Every sentence starts with capital
- [ ] Subject + verb match (she **goes**, not she go)
- [ ] Period at end
- [ ] *I* is capital
- [ ] Read aloud — does it sound right?

---

## Examples

**Stage 1 — 5 sentences about today:**

> I woke up early. I helped my family. I studied English on my phone. I learned ten new words. I feel proud tonight.

**Stage 2 — email (informal):**

>  
> Dear Teacher,

## Self-check questions

1. What is SVO order?
2. When do you capitalize letters?
3. What is a topic sentence?
4. Name three linking words for contrast and reason.
5. What are five items on the editing checklist?

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| i instead of I | Always capital I |
| Run-on sentences (no period) | Split into two sentences |
| Missing articles | Add a/the where needed ([03-core-grammar.md](03-core-grammar.md)) |
| Dari word order | Read aloud — if odd, reorder |
