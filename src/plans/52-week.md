# 52-Week Mastery Path

**Learning goal:** Reach B2-C1 level in one year

**Time:** 30-60 min/day consistently

---

## Quarter 1 (Weeks 1-13): Foundations
- Complete beginner plan
- A1 → A2 level
- 300+ words, basic grammar

## Quarter 2 (Weeks 14-26): Intermediate
- Past, future, modals
- B1 level
- 500-700 words

## Quarter 3 (Weeks 27-39): Upper intermediate
- Complex sentences
- Conditionals, passive voice
- B2 level approaching
- 700-1000 words

## Quarter 4 (Weeks 40-52): Advanced preparation
- Professional/academic English
- Presentations, formal writing
- B2+ or early C1
- 1000+ words

---

## Monthly milestones

**Month 3:** Basic conversations  
**Month 6:** Describe experiences fluently  
**Month 9:** Discuss abstract topics  
**Month 12:** Professional competence

---

## Adjustment points

**Week 13, 26, 39:** Take checkpoint quiz. Adjust pace if needed.

If ahead: Move to next level materials  
If behind: Extend current quarter by 2-4 weeks
