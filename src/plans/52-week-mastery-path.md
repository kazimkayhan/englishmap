# 52-Week Mastery Path (Full Journey)

**30–45 min/day average** · Stages 0 → 4 over **12–24 months** (flexible)

This plan connects:

- Weeks 1–12: [12-week-beginner.md](12-week-beginner.md)
- Weeks 13–36: [24-week-intermediate.md](24-week-intermediate.md) + extension below
- Weeks 37–52: Advanced consolidation

---

## Year map

| Months | Weeks | Stage | Main focus |
|--------|-------|-------|------------|
| 1 | 1–4 | 0→1 | Alphabet, 300 words, present tense |

| 3 | 9–12 | 1→2 | Past intro, Checkpoint 1 complete |

| 10–12 | 37–52 | 3→4 | Professional English, Checkpoint 3–4 |

## Specialization options (Week 49+)

Choose **one** track for vocabulary + reading:

| Track | Read | Write |
|-------|------|-------|
| Academic | Simple Wikipedia science | Summary paragraphs |
| Remote work | Job posts, freelance guides | Cover letter draft |
| Health | WHO simple pages | Health vocabulary journal |
| Technology | Tech blogs (easy) | App review paragraph |
