# 24-Week Intermediate Plan

**Learning goal:** Reach solid B1 level

**Prerequisite:** Complete 12-week beginner plan or place into Week 13

**Time:** 30-45 min/day, 5-6 days/week

---

## Weeks 13-18: Intermediate grammar

- Past tenses mastery (simple, continuous)
- Future forms (will, going to, present continuous)
- Modal verbs (can, should, must, might)
- Present perfect introduction
- Vocabulary to 500 words

---

## Weeks 19-24: Fluency & skills

- Opinions with reasons
- Paragraph writing (8-10 sentences)
- Listening at normal speed
- Speaking 3-5 minutes
- Reading articles (300-500 words)
- Vocabulary to 700 words

---

## Daily routine (45 min)

**Monday:** Vocabulary + pronunciation  
**Tuesday:** Grammar deep dive  
**Wednesday:** Listening (longer clips)  
**Thursday:** Reading + comprehension  
**Friday:** Speaking practice (record)  
**Saturday:** Writing + self-edit  
**Sunday:** Review weak area

---

## Success criteria

By week 24:
- Use past, present, future correctly
- Express opinions with reasons
- Understand most daily conversations
- Speak 5 minutes on familiar topics
- Read news articles with dictionary
- Write formal emails
