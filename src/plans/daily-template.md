# Daily Practice Template

**Learning goal:** Have a clear daily routine to follow

---

## Choose your time block

### 15-minute minimum (busy days)
- 5 min: Review vocabulary
- 5 min: Listen to one clip
- 5 min: Speak 5 sentences aloud

### 30-minute standard
- 5 min: Vocabulary review + 5 new words
- 10 min: Grammar/Writing
- 10 min: Listening
- 5 min: Speaking/Reading aloud

### 45-minute intensive
- 5 min: Review yesterday
- 10 min: Vocabulary (10 new words)
- 10 min: Grammar lesson + exercises
- 10 min: Listening with dictation
- 10 min: Speaking or writing practice

---

## Weekly rotation

Use different focus each day (see [Practice Plans](../13-practice-plans.md))

---

## Tracking

- [ ] Opened materials
- [ ] Completed session
- [ ] Logged in tracker

---

## Flexibility

Bad day? Do 5-10 min. Keep the habit alive.

Good day? Extend by 15 min if energy allows.
