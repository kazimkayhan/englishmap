# 12-Week Beginner Plan

**Learning goal:** Complete beginner foundations (A1-A2)

**Time:** 30-45 min/day, 5-6 days/week

---

## Week-by-week breakdown

### Weeks 1-2: Foundations
- Alphabet & phonics
- First 100 words
- Introduction dialogue
- Present simple (I/you)

### Weeks 3-4: Core grammar
- Be & have
- Present simple (all persons)
- Questions & negatives
- Articles (a, an, the)

### Weeks 5-6: Expanding skills
- Vocabulary to 200 words
- Basic pronunciation (TH, V, W)
- Listening to slow clips
- Speaking: record introduction

### Weeks 7-8: Building fluency
- Vocabulary to 300 words
- Reading short texts
- Writing 5-sentence journal
- Listening without subtitles (try)

### Weeks 9-10: Past tense intro
- Past simple regular verbs
- 10 common irregular verbs
- Story writing (past tense)
- Describing yesterday

### Weeks 11-12: Review & checkpoint
- Review all grammar
- Take unit quizzes
- Checkpoint test
- Plan next 12 weeks

---

## Daily routine template

**Monday:** Vocabulary + pronunciation  
**Tuesday:** Grammar + writing  
**Wednesday:** Listening  
**Thursday:** Reading + review  
**Friday:** Speaking  
**Saturday:** Exercises/drills  
**Sunday:** Rest or light review

---

## Success criteria

By week 12, you should:
- Know 300+ words
- Use present & past simple
- Understand slow English clips
- Speak 1-2 minutes about self
- Read 150-word texts
- Write 100-word paragraphs
