# Consonants for Dari Speakers

**Learning goal:** Pronounce English consonants that don't exist in Dari

---

## Why consonants matter

While vowels are the heart of syllables, **consonants give words their shape**. A few consonant sounds in English don't exist in Dari, and mastering them dramatically improves clarity.

> **Priority sounds:** th, v, p (vs f), w (vs v), and final consonants

---

## Sounds that don't exist in Dari

### Sound 1: TH (two types)

#### Voiceless TH /θ/ — thank, think, three

**How to make it:**
1. Put tongue tip **lightly between your teeth**
2. Blow air out (no voice)
3. You should feel air on your tongue

**Practice words:**  
thank, think, three, thing, nothing, both, month, math

**Common mistake:** Replacing with "t" or "s"
- ❌ "tank" instead of "thank"
- ❌ "sin" instead of "thin"

**Daily drill (2 minutes):**
Say 20 times: "Thank you three times."

---

#### Voiced TH /ð/ — this, that, mother

**How to make it:**
1. Tongue lightly between teeth (same position as /θ/)
2. Add **voice** (vocal cords vibrate)
3. You should feel vibration in your throat

**Practice words:**  
this, that, the, they, mother, father, brother, other, together

**Common mistake:** Replacing with "d" or "z"
- ❌ "dis" instead of "this"
- ❌ "zee" instead of "the"

**Daily drill:**
"This is my mother and that is my father."

---

### Sound 2: V /v/ — very, have, voice

**How to make it:**
1. **Touch upper teeth to lower lip** (not upper lip to lower lip!)
2. Blow air out while adding voice
3. You should feel vibration on your lower lip

**Practice words:**  
very, voice, have, give, love, live, seven, eleven, never, river

**Common mistake:** Replacing with "w" or "f"
- ❌ "wery" instead of "very"
- ❌ "haf" instead of "have"

**Test:** Put your finger on your lower lip — you should feel it vibrate for /v/.

**Daily drill:**
"I have seven very good videos."

> **Dari tip:** Dari has "و" which sounds like /v/ in some contexts, but English /v/ is stronger. Make sure your teeth touch your lip.

---

### Sound 3: W /w/ — water, we, what

**How to make it:**
1. **Round your lips tightly** (like saying "oo")
2. Quickly open to the next vowel
3. **No teeth touching lip** (that's /v/)

**Practice words:**  
water, we, what, where, when, why, one, woman, away, always

**Common mistake:** Confusing with /v/
- ❌ "vater" instead of "water"
- ❌ "vhy" instead of "why"

**Key difference:**
- **V:** teeth on lip, friction
- **W:** lips rounded, no teeth contact

**Daily drill:**
"We want water. Where is it?"

---

### Sound 4: P /p/ (vs F /f/)

**Dari speakers sometimes confuse P and F.**

**P /p/:** Lips together, build air pressure, release with a puff
- **p**en, **p**ay, **p**lease, ha**pp**y, sto**p**

**F /f/:** Teeth on lip, blow air continuously
- **f**an, **f**ast, **f**ood, of**f**er, stu**ff**

**Practice pairs:**
| P | F |
|---|---|
| **p**at | **f**at |
| **p**eel | **f**eel |
| **p**ull | **f**ull |

**Daily drill:**
"Please put the pen on the phone."
"The food is fresh and fast."

---

### Sound 5: Final consonants

**Dari speakers often drop or weaken final consonants. English requires you to finish them.**

#### Final P, T, K (voiceless stops)

**Don't release strongly, but make the shape:**

| Word | How to end it |
|------|---------------|
| sto**p** | Close lips firmly |
| cu**t** | Tongue touches roof of mouth |
| boo**k** | Back of tongue up, slight release |

**Practice:**
- stop, top, cup, help
- cat, it, not, eat, test
- book, look, take, work, like

**Sentence drill:**
"I like that book. Stop at the shop."

---

#### Final D, G, B (voiced stops)

**Same as above, but add voice at the end:**

- wor**d**, goo**d**, nee**d**, rea**d**
- bi**g**, do**g**, le**g**
- jo**b**, we**b**

**Sentence drill:**
"I read a good book about a big dog."

---

#### Final S, Z, SH

- book**s**, cat**s**, take**s** (s = /s/)
- door**s**, tree**s**, give**s** (s = /z/)
- fini**sh**, wi**sh**, pu**sh** (sh)

**Sentence drill:**
"She takes books and pushes doors."

## Consonant clusters (two/three consonants together)

### Initial clusters (at start of words)

English allows consonant clusters that Dari doesn't:

| Cluster | Examples | Dari speaker mistake |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| st- | **st**udy, **st**op, **st**art | Adding vowel: "estudy" |
| sp- | **sp**eak, **sp**end | Adding vowel: "espeak" |
| str- | **str**ong, **str**eet | Adding vowel: "estronG" |
| spr- | **spr**ing, **spr**ead | Very difficult |
| thr- | **thr**ee, **thr**ough | Often becomes "tree" |

**How to fix:** Practice without adding vowel before cluster:
- "study" not "es-tudy"
- "speak" not "es-peak"

**Daily drill:**
"I study English. I speak slowly. I am strong."

---

### Final clusters (at end of words)

| Cluster | Examples | Mistake |
|---------|----------|---------|
| -st | te**st**, mo**st** | Dropping one sound |
| -ld | wor**ld**, co**ld** | "worl" instead of "world" |
| -nd | ha**nd**, fi**nd** | "han" instead of "hand" |
| -nts | stude**nts**, pare**nts** | "studens" |
| -ks | boo**ks**, wee**ks** | Dropping /s/ |

**Key rule:** **Finish every consonant**, even if it feels awkward at first.

**Daily drill:**
"Most students find tests difficult."

## Daily practice routine (5 minutes)

**Monday:** TH voiceless (/θ/) — 20 reps of "thank, think, three"  
**Tuesday:** TH voiced (/ð/) — 20 reps of "this, that, the"  
**Wednesday:** V and W — 10 reps each: "very, voice" / "water, we"  
**Thursday:** Final consonants — 10 words: "stop, cut, book, word, big"  
**Friday:** Initial clusters — 10 words: "study, speak, street, strong"  
**Saturday:** Record a 1-minute speech, listen for errors  
**Sunday:** Rest or light review

## Self-check quiz

1. Where is your tongue for "thank"? *(between teeth)*
2. Where are your teeth for /v/ sound in "very"? *(on lower lip)*
3. Do your lips round for "water"? *(yes)*
4. Say "stop" — do you feel your lips close at the end? *(yes)*
5. Say "students" — do you pronounce all sounds? *(stu-dents, not studens)*

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Example | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| "tank" for "thank" | /θ/ → /t/ | Tongue between teeth, not behind |
| "wery" for "very" | /v/ → /w/ | Teeth on lower lip |
| "vater" for "water" | /w/ → /v/ | Round lips, no teeth contact |
| Dropping final sounds | "tes" for "test" | Exaggerate endings for 1 week |
| "estudy" for "study" | Adding vowel | Start directly with /st/ |

---

## Mirror practice

**Use a mirror to check:**
1. **TH:** Can you see your tongue between your teeth?
2. **V:** Are your upper teeth touching your lower lip?
3. **W:** Are your lips rounded like "oo"?
4. **Final P:** Do your lips close?

---

## Recording practice

1. Record yourself saying these 10 words:
   - thank, this, very, water, stop, test, students, speak, three, world

2. Listen carefully:
   - Did you put your tongue between your teeth for "thank" and "three"?
   - Did you finish all final consonants in "stop," "test," "world"?

3. Re-record once per week — track improvement
