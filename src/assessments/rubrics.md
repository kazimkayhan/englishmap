# Self-Assessment Rubrics

**Learning goal:** Evaluate your own progress honestly

---

## Speaking rubric (A1-C1)

| Level | Can do |
|-------|--------|
| A1 | Introduce self, use simple phrases |
| A2 | Describe daily routine, simple past |
| B1 | Express opinions, tell stories |
| B2 | Discuss abstract topics, argue |
| C1 | Present formally, use nuance |

---

## Writing rubric

Rate yourself 1-5 on:
- Grammar correctness
- Vocabulary range
- Organization/structure
- Clarity of ideas

---

## Listening rubric

- Understand slow English: Yes/Partial/No
- Understand natural speed: Yes/Partial/No
- Catch details without replay: Yes/Partial/No

---

## Use rubrics monthly

Track progress over time. Celebrate improvements!
