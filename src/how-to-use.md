# How to Use This Book

This digital book is designed to be your complete English learning companion. Whether you're reading on a phone, tablet, or computer, here's how to get the most from it.

---

## Navigation

### Sidebar (Left Panel)
- Click/tap any chapter title to jump to that section
- Chapters with ▸ can be expanded to show subsections
- Your current page is highlighted
- Collapsible sections help you focus on one area at a time

### Top Bar
- 🔍 **Search** — find any word or topic instantly
- 🎨 **Theme** — switch between light and dark mode
- 🖨️ **Print** — print individual chapters or the whole book
- ☰ **Menu** — toggle sidebar on mobile

### Bottom Navigation
- ← **Previous** and **Next** → buttons at the bottom of each page
- Progress through the book in order, or jump around as needed

## Special Features

### Bilingual Support
- **English** is the main teaching language
- **Dari companion** pages (دری) explain concepts in your first language
- **Hazaragi notes** (هزارگی) show pronunciation and vocabulary differences
- Use these when English explanations aren't clear

### Exercises & Practice
- Each skill chapter has linked exercises
- Try exercises **before** checking answer keys
- Answer keys are in a separate section to avoid spoilers
- Mark completed exercises in your tracker

### Offline Use
Once you load this book:
- All pages are cached on your device
- You can read without internet
- Perfect for intermittent connectivity
- Search works offline too

### Mobile-Friendly
- Designed for small screens
- Short, focused sections (5-15 min reading)
- Collapsible menu saves screen space
- Readable fonts and spacing

## Study Modes

### Daily Study (30-45 min)
1. Pick one skill area for the day
2. Read the lesson (10-15 min)
3. Do the exercises (15-20 min)
4. Review or record yourself (10 min)

### Weekend Deep Dive (1-2 hours)
1. Choose a complete chapter
2. Read thoroughly with notes
3. Do all related exercises
4. Check answers and identify weak spots
5. Redo problem areas

### Quick Review (15 min)
1. Use Search to find a specific topic
2. Skim the lesson
3. Do 3-5 practice items
4. Move on

### On a Private Device

## Tips for Success

### 1. **Read in Order First Time**
- If you're new, follow the book structure
- Each chapter builds on previous ones
- Skip only if placement test says to

### 2. **Use Search Liberally**
- Can't remember where articles are explained?
- Forgot the word "shadowing"?
- Search finds it instantly

### 3. **Switch Languages When Stuck**
- English explanation unclear? → Read Dari companion
- Pronunciation confusing? → Check Hazaragi notes
- Still stuck? → Post question or review chapter later

### 4. **Print/Save Key Pages**
- Study plans work well printed
- Worksheets can be printed and filled by hand
- Trackers work great in a notebook

### 5. **Adjust to Your Device**
- Phone → use collapsed sidebar, short sessions
- Tablet → keep sidebar open, longer sessions
- Computer → print-friendly view for PDF export

## Customizing Your Experience

### Light vs Dark Theme
- **Light** — better for daylight reading
- **Dark** — easier on eyes at night or in low light
- Switch anytime with the theme button

### Text Size
- Your browser's zoom (Ctrl/Cmd +/-) works
- Mobile: pinch to zoom on any page

### Printing
- Click print icon for clean, printer-friendly layout
- Save as PDF to keep offline copies
- Great for worksheets and study plans
