# Intermediate Growth (B1 → B2)

**Stage 2** · Months 4–8

---

## Learning goals

1. Use **past tense** for stories and experiences
2. Use **future** forms (will, going to, present continuous for plans)
3. Express **opinions** and **reasons**
4. Connect ideas with **linking words**
5. Speak and write **2–5 minutes** on familiar topics

---

## Why this skill matters

Beginner English describes *now*. Intermediate English describes *your life story* and *your plans* — past hardships, future dreams, present choices.

> *You are no longer “studying English.” You are using English to live and think.*

---

## Level focus

**Stage 2** (B1 → B2 bridge).

**Prerequisite:** Solid Stage 1 — present tense, 300+ words, basic listening.

---

## Step-by-step lessons

### Lesson 1 — Past simple: regular verbs (20 min)

**Form:** verb + **-ed** (walk → walked)

| Affirmative | Negative | Question |
|-------------|----------|----------|
| I walked | I did not walk | Did you walk? |
| She studied | She did not study | Did she study? |

**Spelling rules:**

- stop → stopped (double consonant)
- try → tried (y → ied)
- play → played

**Examples:**

> Yesterday I **studied** English for forty minutes.  
> Last year I **started** learning at home.  
> She **helped** her sister with homework.

---

### Lesson 2 — Past simple: irregular verbs (25 min)

Learn in **groups of 5 per week**:

| Base | Past |
|------|------|
| go | went |
| see | saw |
| make | made |
| take | took |
| come | came |
| get | got |
| know | knew |
| think | thought |
| say | said |
| feel | felt |
| have | had |
| be | was / were |

**Story practice:**

> Last week I **went** online. I **saw** a free course. I **took** notes. I **felt** nervous but happy.

---

### Lesson 3 — Past continuous (15 min)

**Was/were + verb-ing** — action in progress in the past

> I **was studying** when my mother called.  
> They **were watching** a lesson at 8 p.m.

**Contrast:**

> I **studied** (finished action) vs I **was studying** (in progress)

---

### Lesson 4 — Future forms (20 min)

| Form | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| **will** | decision, promise, prediction | I **will** practice tomorrow. |
| **going to** | plan already decided | I am **going to** finish Module 10. |
| **present continuous** | fixed arrangement | I am **meeting** my sister tonight to study. |

---

### Lesson 5 — Modals for ability and advice (20 min)

| Modal | Meaning | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| can / could | ability | I **can** read slow English. |
| should | advice | You **should** review old words. |
| must / have to | necessity | I **must** rest when I am sick. |
| may / might | possibility | It **might** rain; I will study inside. |

---

### Lesson 6 — Opinions (15 min)

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| I think… | I think daily practice works. |
| In my opinion… | In my opinion, women should have education. |
| I believe… | I believe hope is important. |
| I agree / disagree | I disagree with stopping after one hard day. |

**Structure:** Opinion + reason + example

> **I think** English opens opportunities. **Because** many courses are online. **For example**, I found free grammar lessons.

---

### Lesson 7 — Connectors and storytelling (20 min)

**Timeline words:** first, then, after that, later, finally

**Contrast:** however, although, but

**Cause:** because, since, so

**Mini story (90 sec spoken):**

> **First**, I was afraid to speak. **Then** I recorded my voice every week. **After that**, I understood more listening clips. **Finally**, I spoke for two minutes without stopping. **Although** I still make mistakes, I continue.

---

### Lesson 8 — Fluency bridges (15 min)

**Fillers (natural, not too many):**

- Well, … / Let me see, … / I mean, …

**Rephrase when stuck:**

> Sorry, let me say that another way. …  
> What I want to say is, …

**Practice:** 2-minute talk on *a challenge I overcame*.

---

## Examples

**Past + opinion paragraph:**

> Last month I had a difficult week. I did not study for three days because I was tired. However, I started again on Monday. I think consistency matters more than perfection. I am going to study thirty minutes daily this month. I believe small steps change lives.

## Self-check questions

1. How do you form past tense for regular verbs?
2. Past of *go*, *see*, *have*?
3. When use *will* vs *going to*?
4. Write one opinion + because + example.
5. Name four timeline connectors.

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| I go yesterday | I **went** yesterday |
| Did you went? | Did you **go**? |
| I will going | I **am going to** go / I **will** go |
| No connector in long speech | Add first, then, finally |
