# Vocabulary Building

**Stage 1 → Stage 2** · Ongoing daily habit

---

## Learning goals

1. Learn **high-frequency words** in themed groups
2. Use **word families** (act → action → active)
3. Apply **spaced repetition** without paid apps
4. Reach **300 words** (month 2) and **800+** (month 6)
5. Connect new words to **speaking and writing** same day

---

## Why this skill matters

Grammar without words is an empty house. Vocabulary lets you say what you **feel** and **need**.

> *Ten new words today, used in one sentence each, beat fifty words memorized silently.*

---

## Level focus

**Stage 1–3** (never stop learning words).

---

## Step-by-step lessons

### Lesson 1 — How to learn a new word (15 min)

For each word, save **4 parts**:

| Part | Example: *patient* |
|------|---------------------|
| English | patient |
| Meaning (simple) | able to wait calmly |
| Example sentence | I am patient with my English. |
| Pronunciation note | PAY-shunt (stress first syllable) |

**Same day:** speak the sentence aloud.

---

### Lesson 2 — Top 50 verbs (20 min)

be, have, do, say, get, make, go, know, take, see, come, think, look, want, give, use, find, tell, ask, work, seem, feel, try, leave, call, need, become, keep, let, begin, help, show, hear, play, run, move, live, believe, bring, happen, write, sit, stand, lose, pay, meet, include, continue, set, learn, change

---

### Lesson 3 — Top 50 nouns for your life (20 min)

time, year, people, way, day, woman, man, child, world, life, hand, part, place, work, week, case, point, government, company, number, night, home, water, room, mother, father, area, money, story, fact, month, lot, right, study, book, eye, job, word, business, issue, side, kind, head, house, service, friend, father, power, hour, game, line, end, member, health, idea, food, student, teacher, language, phone, internet, message, problem, answer, question

---

### Lesson 4 — Thematic sets (learn 10/week)

**Home & daily life:** kitchen, wash, clean, cook, sleep, wake, tired, busy, quiet, safe

**Study:** course, lesson, practice, test, mistake, improve, goal, plan, notebook, review

**Health:** doctor, medicine, pain, better, rest, exercise, body, heart, sick, well

**Technology:** download, app, Wi-Fi, password, screen, charge, battery, click, search, online

**Emotions & strength:** hope, courage, proud, worry, calm, strong, free, dream, choose, try again

---

### Lesson 5 — Word families (15 min)

| Root | Family |
|------|--------|
| study | student, studied, studying |
| help | helpful, helper, helpless (careful: negative meaning) |
| create | creative, creation, creator |
| educate | education, educational, educator |

**Rule:** Learn the verb first, then nouns/adjectives.

---

### Lesson 6 — Spaced repetition (phone method) (15 min)

**Day 1:** Learn 10 words  
**Day 2:** Review Day 1 + learn 10 new  
**Day 4:** Review all  
**Day 7:** Review all  
**Day 14:** Review all  

**Tool:** Notes app — title `English words Week 3`. Move mastered words to `DONE` section.

---

### Lesson 7 — 1000-word path (overview)

| Month | Target active words |
|-------|---------------------|
| 1 | 150 |
| 2 | 300 |
| 4 | 500 |
| 6 | 800 |
| 12 | 1200+ |

“Active” = you can use it in a sentence, not only recognize it.

## Self-check questions

1. What four parts should every new word entry have?
2. What is spaced repetition?
3. Give one word family from *create*.
4. How many new words per day is realistic on a busy week?
5. Why use new words in sentences the same day?

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Only translating word lists | Always add an English sentence |
| Too many words per day | 5–10 new words is enough |
| Ignoring pronunciation | Note stress on first day |
| Separating vocab from grammar | Use new words in grammar drills |
