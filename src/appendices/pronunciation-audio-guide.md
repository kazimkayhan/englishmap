## How to use this guide (daily 10 min)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Pick **one letter or sound** from the table below |
| 2 | Open **Forvo** or **YouTube** link — listen 5 times |
| 3 | Say aloud 10 times — record on phone |
| 4 | Compare recording to model (not for shame — for data) |
| 5 | Tick box in [worksheet-phonics-blends.md](../assets/worksheets/worksheet-phonics-blends.md) |

**Master site for single words:** https://forvo.com/search/{word}/en/us/  
Example: https://forvo.com/search/hello/en/us/

## Critical sounds for Dari speakers

| Sound | How to make it | Words | Forvo bundle (open each) |
|-------|----------------|-------|---------------------------|
| **th** voiceless | Tongue between teeth, air out | thank, three, think | thank · three · think |
| **th** voiced | Tongue between teeth, voice on | this, that, mother | this · mother |
| **v** | Top teeth on lower lip | very, have, live | very · have |
| **p vs f** | lips vs teeth-on-lip | pat/fat, pen/fen | pat · fat |
| **short i vs long ee** | sit vs seat | ship/sheep | ship · sheep |
| **final consonants** | Finish the sound | help, want, study | help · want |

Links:  
- https://forvo.com/search/thank/en/us/  
- https://forvo.com/search/this/en/us/  
- https://forvo.com/search/sheep/en/us/  
- https://forvo.com/search/ship/en/us/

## Mouth & voice tips

- **th:** tongue visible between teeth lightly  
- **v:** bite lower lip gently  
- **r (American):** tongue not rolled; lips slightly round  
- Drink water — dry mouth hides sounds  
- Speak **louder than whisper** for practice
