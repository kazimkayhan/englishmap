## What fluency means here

Fluency is **smooth, understandable speech** — not zero mistakes. You can be fluent at B1 and still learn until C2.

> *Fluency grows when your mouth practices English as often as your eyes.*

---

## The 4 fluency engines

| Engine | Daily action | Minutes |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| Input | Listen to clear English | 10 |
| Output | Speak aloud (self-talk) | 10 |
| Feedback | Record + listen once | 5 |
| Review | Repeat yesterday’s phrases | 5 |

---

## Week 1–4 fluency challenge (beginner)

| Week | Challenge |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Say 5 sentences about morning — no writing |
| 2 | Shadow 10 lines from slow audio |
| 3 | 60-sec voice note daily |
| 4 | Describe a photo in 8 sentences |

---

## Week 5–12 (intermediate)

| Week | Challenge |
|------|-----------|
| 5–6 | Tell past story 90 sec |
| 7–8 | Opinion + because + example 2 min |
| 9–10 | Summarize a short article aloud |
| 11–12 | Role-play both sides of a dialogue |

---

## Connector pack (paste in Notes)

First, … / Then, … / After that, … / However, … / Because, … / For example, … / In my opinion, … / Finally, …

**Drill:** Use 4 connectors in one 2-minute talk.

---

## When you freeze

1. Say: *Let me think.*  
2. Say idea in **simple** words.  
3. Use gesture if with sister (optional).  
4. Do **not** switch to Dari mid-sentence if goal is English mode.

## Milestones

| Level | Fluency sign |
|-------|----------------|
| A1 | 30 sec intro smooth |
| A2 | 1 min routine |
| B1 | 2 min story |
| B2 | 3 min opinion |
| C1 | 5 min structured talk |
