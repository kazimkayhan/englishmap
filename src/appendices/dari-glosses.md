## Grammar

| English | Dari hint | Note |
|---------|-----------|------|
| **a / an** | یک — general one | English requires *a book*, not just *book* |
| **the** | آن / همان — specific | When both know which thing |
| **am / is / are** | هست — but English **never drops** | Wrong: *I fine* → *I am fine* |
| **have / has** | داشتن | *I have time* = وقت دارم |
| **do / does** (questions) | — | Helper verb; Dari uses different question form |
| **-s** (he goes) | — | Third person always adds **-s** in present |
| **there is / are** | وجود دارد | *There is a book* = کتاب هست |

## Pronunciation

| Sound | Dari note | Practice word |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **th** (thank) | Between teeth; not ت or س | thank, three |
| **th** (this) | Voiced; vibration | this, mother |
| **v** | شبیه و in some words | very — teeth on lip |
| **p vs f** | پ vs ف | pat vs fat |
| **short i vs long ee** | ش vs شی | ship vs sheep |

## Word order

| English pattern | Dari habit |
|-----------------|------------|
| Subject – Verb – Object | I study English |
| Question: Do you…? | آیا تو …؟ — but build: **Do** + you + verb |
| Adjective before noun | **red** book (not book red) |
