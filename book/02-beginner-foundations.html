EnglishMap — English for Dari & Hazaragi Speakers

Beginner Foundations

Stage 0 → Stage 1 start · Weeks 1–4

Learning goals

After this module, you will:

  1. Read and write the English alphabet (upper and lower case)
  2. Type simple English on a smartphone keyboard
  3. Use 100 survival words in context
  4. Say basic phrases: hello, thanks, help, numbers 1–20
  5. Feel confident enough to open 03-core-grammar.md

Why this skill matters

Every house needs a floor. Alphabet and survival English are your floor. You can greet someone, read simple signs, and start grammar without fear.

Day 1 win: spell your name in English letters.

Level focus

Stage 0 (pre-beginner) and early Stage 1.

Step-by-step lessons

Lesson 1 — The alphabet (20 min)

English has 26 letters. Names are capitalized: Fatima, Kabul.

Vowels: A E I O U (sometimes Y)

Consonants: B C D F G H J K L M N P Q R S T V W X Y Z

Practice: Say each letter aloud. Write: A a B b C c …

Phone tip: Switch keyboard to English (US). Long-press globe icon.

Dari note: English letters look different from Arabic script. Your eyes will adapt in 1–2 weeks with daily practice.

Lesson 2 — Sounds that match letters (15 min)

Most letters have one common sound:

LetterExample word
Bbook
Ffood
Mmother

Some are special (learn more in 05-pronunciation.md):

  • Th — thank, this
  • Sh — she
  • Ch — child

Lesson 3 — First 50 words (20 min)

Learn in groups of 10 per day.

Group 1 — People

EnglishMeaning idea
II
youyou
hehe
sheshe
wewe
theythey
manman
womanwoman
childchild
familyfamily

Group 2 — Home

house, room, door, water, food, bread, tea, day, night, home

Group 3 — Actions

go, come, eat, drink, read, write, see, hear, want, need

Group 4 — Good / bad

good, bad, big, small, hot, cold, happy, sad, yes, no

Group 5 — Connectors

and, but, in, on, here, there, now, today, please, thank you

Lesson 4 — Numbers and time (15 min)

1–20: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty

Useful phrases:

  • What time is it?
  • today / tomorrow / yesterday
  • morning / afternoon / evening

Lesson 5 — Survival dialogues (20 min)

Dialogue A — Meeting

  • A: Hello. My name is Sara.
  • B: Hi, Sara. I am Mary. Nice to meet you.
  • A: Nice to meet you too.

Dialogue B — Thanks

  • A: Thank you for your help.
  • B: You are welcome.

Dialogue C — Help

  • Excuse me. I need help, please.
  • I do not understand. Can you speak slowly?

Dialogue D — Health (simple)

  • I am not well today.
  • I need water, please.

Practice: Read aloud 3 times. Record on phone. Listen once.

Lesson 6 — Second 50 words (20 min)

study, learn, English, book, phone, internet, message, friend, sister, work, school, doctor, health, problem, question, answer, open, close, wait, stop, start, look, listen, speak, say, tell, know, think, like, love, live, city, country, world, name, age, year, month, week, hour, minute, money, free, new, old, right, left, up, down

Examples

Introduce yourself:

Hello. My name is Zahra. I am 22. I live in Afghanistan. I study English at home. I want to learn for my future. Thank you.

Short message (like chat):

Good morning. I am fine today. I study English for 30 minutes. See you later.

Practice tasks

  1. Copy the alphabet twice in your notes app.
  2. Learn 10 words from Group 1 — test yourself tonight.
  3. Record Dialogue A on your phone.
  4. Write your name and city in English.
  5. Full drills: exercises/02-beginner-drills.md

Self-check questions

  1. How many letters are in the English alphabet?
  2. Write the numbers 7, 11, and 15 in English words.
  3. Complete: “My name ___ Sara.” (choose: am / is / are — check grammar module if unsure)
  4. What do you say when someone helps you?
  5. Can you read your introduction aloud without stopping?

Common mistakes

MistakeFix
Mixing Dari script in English sentencesUse Latin letters only in English practice
Silent practice onlySpeak aloud every day
Learning 100 words in one day10 words/day is stronger
Skipping reviewReview yesterday before new words

Extended practice

Dialogue E — At the door

Guest: Excuse me. Is anyone home?
You: Yes. Please come in. Can I help you?
Guest: Thank you. I am looking for your mother.
You: Please wait. I will call her.

Dialogue F — Study

A: Do you study English?
B: Yes, I study at home every day.
A: That is wonderful. Keep going.

Word build: Add 10 family words: aunt, uncle, cousin, grandmother, grandfather, husband, wife, baby, parent, relative (use dictionary once).
