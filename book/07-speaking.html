EnglishMap — English for Dari & Hazaragi Speakers

Speaking Skills

Stage 1 → Stage 4 · Speak aloud every study day

Learning goals

  1. Speak daily even without a partner
  2. Use shadowing (repeat with audio)
  3. Build self-talk habits (describe your day)
  4. Record voice safely on your phone
  5. Grow from survival phrases to 2+ minute talks

Why this skill matters

English lives in your mouth, not only in your notebook. Speaking builds fluency and courage faster than silent study.

Your voice is valid even with mistakes. Communication beats perfection.

Level focus

Stage 1: phrases · Stage 2: 1–2 min · Stage 3+: opinions, stories

Step-by-step lessons

Lesson 1 — Speak from day 1 (10 min)

Rules:

  • Louder than whisper — your mouth learns muscle memory
  • Slow is fine — clear beats fast
  • Mistakes allowed — fix one thing per day, not ten

Today: Say your introduction 5 times (from 02-beginner-foundations.md).

Lesson 2 — Shadowing (15 min)

  1. Play one sentence (slow audio)
  2. Pause
  3. Repeat exactly — copy rhythm and stress
  4. Record yourself
  5. Compare once

Starter sentences:

  • I am learning English step by step.
  • Today I feel hopeful about my future.
  • I study at home because I want a better life.

Lesson 3 — Self-talk (15 min)

Narrate your real day in simple English:

I wake up. I drink tea. I open my phone. I study English. I help my mother. I feel tired but happy.

Levels:

StageSelf-talk length
15 sentences
210 sentences, past tense
3opinions + reasons
4abstract topics (hope, education, work)

Lesson 4 — Conversation patterns (20 min)

Agree: I agree. / That is true. / Me too.

Disagree politely: I see your point, but I think…

Ask more: Can you tell me more? / Why do you think that?

Buy time: Let me think… / That is a good question.

Practice alone: Play both sides — A and B voice.

Lesson 5 — Recording safely on smartphone (10 min)

  • Folder name: English practice private
  • Delete old files monthly
  • Do not post publicly without strong reason
  • If sharing with sister/friend: trusted person only

Weekly challenge: 60-second voice note — topic: my week.

Lesson 6 — Optional peer practice (safety first)

If you use apps or chat:

  • No personal address or school location
  • Public place not required for voice chat — home is fine
  • Stop if someone is rude or pressures you
  • Prefer female language partners when possible
  • See safe app list in 15-resources.md

Examples

Stage 1 — 30 seconds:

Hello. My name is Maryam. I am 20 years old. I live in Afghanistan. I study English every day at home. I want to work online in the future. Thank you for listening.

Stage 2 — 90 seconds (outline):

  • Past: Yesterday I studied grammar.
  • Present: Today I feel motivated.
  • Future: Tomorrow I will practice listening.
  • Opinion: I think English opens doors for women.

Practice tasks

  1. Shadow 5 sentences from any listening clip.
  2. Self-talk: describe breakfast to bedtime (10 sentences).
  3. Record 60-second introduction; listen once.
  4. Drills: exercises/07-speaking-drills.md

Self-check questions

  1. What is shadowing?
  2. Why is self-talk useful without a teacher?
  3. Name two phrases for polite disagreement.
  4. How should you store recordings on a shared phone?
  5. What is a good Stage 1 speaking goal length?

Common mistakes

MistakeFix
Silent reading onlySpeak 5 min before ending session
Speaking too fastSlow down — clarity first
Ashamed of accentFocus on one sound per week
Skipping recordingRecord weekly to hear growth

Study habits

  • Speak before you feel ready
  • Same topic 3 days — Monday introduce, Wednesday add detail, Friday smoother
  • Celebrate: “I spoke today” ✓ in tracker
