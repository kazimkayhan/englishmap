EnglishMap — English for Dari & Hazaragi Speakers

Free and Low-Cost Resources

American English · Smartphone-first · Updated for self-study

Learning goals

  1. Find trusted free apps and websites
  2. Build an offline study pack
  3. Choose safe American media
  4. Avoid scams and unsafe language-exchange situations
  5. Match resources to your stage

Why resources matter

Good tools save time. Bad tools waste data and hope. This list favors free, reputable, offline-friendly options.

One app downloaded well beats ten apps opened once.

Offline pack (download on Wi-Fi)

Create folder: English offline

  • 5 slow audio files (5–10 min each)
  • 1 PDF or saved page: word list from 04-vocabulary-building.md
  • This roadmap’s modules copied to phone storage
  • 2 exercise files + hide keys elsewhere
  • Tracker template from assets/tracker-template.md

Reuse same files all week — depth over novelty.

Writing feedback without a teacher

  1. Checklist in 09-writing.md

  3. Compare to model paragraphs in modules
  4. Sister circle peer review (12-study-strategies.md)
  5. Record → listen for grammar you know

Low-cost options (if budget allows later)

  • One grammar book (American edition) — used copy OK
  • Italki single lessons (choose verified tutors; safety rules apply)
  • Coursera / edX audit free courses in English

Not required for success with this roadmap.

Safety online

  • No paid “guaranteed fluent in 7 days” scams
  • Do not send money to unknown tutors on social media
  • Protect email and phone in apps
  • Report/block rude partners immediately
  • Prefer learning content over random chat with strangers

Dari / bilingual note

Full Dari translation of this roadmap is not included (per plan). Use Dari only to understand a concept once, then return to English practice. Bilingual subtitles sometimes help understanding — switch to English subtitles as soon as possible.

Practice tasks

  1. Install 1–2 apps max — not ten.
  2. Download 3 VOA clips offline.
  3. Save this repo’s next module to phone files.
  4. Bookmark VOA + one grammar site.

Self-check questions

  1. Why not use ten apps at once?
  2. What goes in an offline pack?
  3. Name two American slow-news sources.
  4. What is the subtitle progression?
  5. What safety rule applies to HelloTalk?
