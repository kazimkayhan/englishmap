Placement Quiz

Find your starting level in 20 minutes

This quiz helps you discover where to begin in EnglishMap. Try your best — guessing is okay, but don't look up answers or use translation tools.

Instructions

  1. Do all 25 questions (or as many as you can)
  2. Check your answers in Answer Keys after finishing
  3. Count correct answers and see your recommended starting point below
  4. Be honest — placing yourself correctly saves time

Section 1: Vocabulary (10 questions)

Choose the correct word:

1. Hello, my _____ is Sara.

  • a) name
  • b) house
  • c) day

2. I _____ English every day.

  • a) eat
  • b) study
  • c) water

3. The opposite of "good" is _____.

  • a) happy
  • b) bad
  • c) big

4. She _____ water.

  • a) drink
  • b) drinks
  • c) drinking

5. I am _____ years old.

  • a) twenty
  • b) hungry
  • c) student

6. They _____ to school.

  • a) go
  • b) goes
  • c) going

7. This is my _____ (place where I sleep).

  • a) book
  • b) food
  • c) room

8. I _____ happy today.

  • a) is
  • b) am
  • c) are

9. She _____ two sisters.

  • a) have
  • b) has
  • c) having

10. We _____ English at home.

  • a) learns
  • b) learning
  • c) learn

Section 2: Grammar (10 questions)

Choose the correct sentence:

11.

  • a) I am student.
  • b) I am a student.
  • c) I student.

12.

  • a) She have a book.
  • b) She has a book.
  • c) She having a book.

13.

  • a) They are happy.
  • b) They is happy.
  • c) They be happy.

14.

  • a) Do you speak English?
  • b) You speak English?
  • c) Speak you English?

15.

  • a) I don't have a phone.
  • b) I no have a phone.
  • c) I not have a phone.

16. Yesterday, I _____ to the market.

  • a) go
  • b) went
  • c) going

17. She _____ a doctor in the future.

  • a) will be
  • b) is
  • c) was

18. I _____ learning English for two months.

  • a) am
  • b) have been
  • c) was

19. If I _____ time, I will study.

  • a) have
  • b) had
  • c) will have

20. The book _____ by her last week.

  • a) write
  • b) was written
  • c) is writing

Section 3: Reading Comprehension (5 questions)

Read this passage:

My name is Layla. I am 20 years old. I live in Kabul with my family. I study English at home every day for 30 minutes. I want to learn English because it will help me get a job online. My favorite subject is vocabulary. I listen to English podcasts and read simple articles. Sometimes it is difficult, but I do not give up.

21. Where does Layla live?

  • a) Herat
  • b) Kabul
  • c) Kandahar

22. How long does Layla study English each day?

  • a) 10 minutes
  • b) 30 minutes
  • c) 2 hours

23. Why does Layla want to learn English?

  • a) To travel
  • b) To get a job online
  • c) To teach others

24. What is Layla's favorite English subject?

  • a) Grammar
  • b) Vocabulary
  • c) Pronunciation

25. What does Layla say about difficulty?

  • a) She gives up
  • b) She asks for help
  • c) She does not give up

Next Steps

After Scoring

  1. Note your level in your Progress Tracker
  2. Start reading the recommended chapter today
  3. Choose a study plan:
    • 0-8 → 12-Week Beginner Plan
    • 9-16 → 24-Week Intermediate Plan
    • 17+ → 52-Week Mastery Path
  4. Retake this quiz every 8-12 weeks to measure progress

If You Scored Lower Than Expected

Don't worry! This is your starting point, not your limit.

  • Everyone starts somewhere
  • Honest placement = faster progress
  • Review is powerful — you'll move through early stages quickly

If You Scored Higher Than Expected

Great!

  • Skim earlier chapters to fill any gaps
  • Start at your recommended level
  • Adjust up if it feels too easy after 1-2 weeks
