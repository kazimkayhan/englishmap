EnglishMap — English for Dari & Hazaragi Speakers

High-Frequency Words (1-300)

Learning goal: Master the 300 most common English words

Why these 300 words matter

These 300 words make up about 65% of all written English. Learn them well, and you can:

  • Understand basic texts
  • Have simple conversations
  • Express most daily needs
  • Build a foundation for grammar

Study strategy: Learn 10-15 words per day with example sentences. Review daily.

How to use this list

For each word:

  1. Read the English word
  2. Check the Dari meaning
  3. Study the example sentence
  4. Write your own sentence
  5. Say the sentence aloud

Don't just memorize translations. Use each word in a sentence the same day you learn it.

Full word list

See the complete list with Dari translations and examples in: Appendix: First 300 High-Frequency Words

That appendix includes:

  • All 300 words with Dari translations (دری)
  • Example sentence for each word
  • Words grouped by type (verb, noun, adjective, etc.)

Learning groups (suggested 20-day plan)

Days 1-2: Group 1 — Core pronouns & be (words 1-30)

I, you, he, she, it, we, they, be, have, do, say, get, make, go, know, take, see, come, think, look, want, give, use, find, tell, ask, work, need, feel, try

Practice: Write 5 sentences about yourself using these words.

Days 3-4: Group 2 — Essential verbs (words 31-60)

call, keep, let, begin, help, show, hear, play, run, move, live, believe, bring, happen, write, sit, stand, lose, pay, meet, include, continue, set, learn, change, lead, understand, watch, follow, stop

Practice: Describe your morning routine using 8 verbs.

Days 5-6: Group 3 — Common nouns — time & life (words 61-90)

time, year, people, way, day, man, world, life, hand, part, child, place, woman, work, week, case, point, number, group, problem, fact, month, lot, right, study, book, eye, job, word, business

Practice: Write about your day using 10 time/life words.

Days 7-8: Group 4 — More nouns — things & places (words 91-120)

issue, side, kind, head, house, service, friend, power, hour, game, line, end, member, law, car, city, name, president, minute, idea, body, information, water, room, mother, area, money, story, family, student

Practice: Describe your home and family using 10 words.

Days 9-10: Group 5 — Adjectives & states (words 121-150)

good, new, first, last, long, great, little, own, other, old, right, big, high, different, small, large, next, early, young, important, few, public, bad, same, able

Practice: Describe 5 things/people with adjectives.

Days 11-12: Group 6 — Connection words & more (words 151-180)

and, or, but, not, all, some, if, when, so, up, out, about, over, after, more, most, only, very, well, back, much, good, very, really, just, still, also, then

Practice: Write 8 sentences using "and," "but," "so," "when."

Days 13-14: Group 7 — Actions & states continued (words 181-210)

go, come, take, get, make, see, know, think, say, find, give, tell, ask, work, feel, become, leave, put, mean, keep, let, begin, seem, help, talk, turn, start, might, show, hear

Practice: Write 10 action sentences about your week.

Days 15-16: Group 8 — Questions & directions (words 211-240)

what, which, who, where, when, why, how, there, here, this, that, these, those, one, two, three, all, both, each, every, many, some, few, any, no, other, another

Practice: Ask 10 questions using question words.

Days 17-18: Group 9 — Prepositions & location (words 241-270)

in, on, at, to, for, of, with, from, by, about, as, into, through, during, before, after, above, below, under, over, between, among, around, near, far, up, down

Practice: Describe where 5 things are (in, on, under, near...).

Days 19-20: Group 10 — Final essentials (words 271-300)

can, will, would, could, should, may, might, must, shall, yes, no, ok, please, thank, sorry, hello, goodbye, help, stop, wait, listen, speak, read, write, open, close, begin, end, start, finish

Practice: Write polite requests using "please," "can," "could."

Study methods

Method 1: Flashcards (phone notes)

  • Line 1: English word
  • Line 2: Dari meaning + example
  • Review 20 words before bed

Method 2: Spaced repetition

  • Day 1: Learn 15 words
  • Day 2: Review Day 1 + learn 15 new
  • Day 4: Review all from Days 1-2
  • Day 7: Review all
  • Day 14: Final review

Method 3: Thematic sentences

Write one paragraph (8-10 sentences) using as many words from each group as possible.

Example (Group 1 words):

I am a student. I study English every day. I want to learn. I think it is important. I see my future. I have hope.

Method 4: Daily practice

  • Morning: Review yesterday's 15 words
  • Midday: Learn 15 new words
  • Evening: Use 5 new words in spoken sentences

Self-check quiz

After learning each group of 30 words:

  1. Can you write 10 of them from memory?
  2. Can you use 5 of them in sentences?
  3. Can you recognize them when you read them?

If yes to all three → move to next group.
If no → review for 2 more days before moving on.

Common mistakes

MistakeFix
Only memorizing translationsWrite example sentences in English
Learning 50 words in one dayStick to 10-15 per day for retention
Not reviewingReview yesterday's words before learning new ones
Silent study onlySay words aloud, use them in speech

Integration with grammar

As you learn words 1-300, practice them with grammar patterns:

  • Words 1-50: Practice with "be" (am/is/are)
  • Words 51-100: Practice with "have" and present simple
  • Words 101-150: Practice in questions
  • Words 151-200: Practice with negatives
  • Words 201-250: Practice with prepositions
  • Words 251-300: Practice with modal verbs (can, will, would)

Tracking progress

Create a simple tracker in your notes:

Week 1: ✅ Words 1-75 (Groups 1-2.5)
Week 2: ✅ Words 76-150 (Groups 2.5-5)
Week 3: 🔲 Words 151-225 (Groups 6-7.5)
Week 4: 🔲 Words 226-300 (Groups 7.5-10)

Next steps

Once you master words 1-300:

  1. Move to High-Frequency Words (301-700)
  2. Practice with Memory Techniques
  3. Use words in Speaking and Writing
