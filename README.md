# English Learning Roadmap

**From zero to mastery — self-study for young women learning American English**

---

## Who this is for

This roadmap is built for **young women (about 18–25)** in Afghanistan who are learning English as a second language. Your first language is **Dari**. You may study alone, at home, on a **smartphone**, for about **30–45 minutes a day**.

You do not need a classroom or a paid teacher. You do need patience, courage, and small daily steps.

> *Your education was interrupted. Your future is not.*

---

## What you will learn

| Skill | What you will be able to do |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| Speaking | Introduce yourself, share opinions, hold longer conversations |
| Listening | Understand slow and natural American English |
| Reading | Read messages, articles, and study materials |
| Writing | Write clear messages, paragraphs, and formal text |
| Grammar | Build correct American English sentences |
| Vocabulary | Use 1,000+ high-frequency words confidently |
| Pronunciation | Speak clearly enough to be understood |
| Fluency | Think and respond without long pauses |
| Confidence | Study even after breaks — without shame |
| Self-study | Plan, track, and fix your own mistakes |

## Your learning path (five stages)

```
Stage 0  →  Pre-beginner     (weeks 1–2)
Stage 1  →  Beginner A1–A2   (months 1–3)
Stage 2  →  Intermediate B1  (months 4–8)
Stage 3  →  Upper B2–C1      (months 9–14)
Stage 4  →  Advanced C1–C2   (months 15–24+)
```

At 30–45 minutes per day, expect about **3 months** to finish strong beginner skills, and **18–24 months** to reach advanced professional English — faster if you study more, and **still successful** if life slows you down.

## Principles of this roadmap

1. **Safety first** — Study in private when you need to. Protect voice recordings and passwords.
2. **Respect** — You are a capable adult learner, not a child.
3. **Low pressure** — Missing days is normal. Restart without guilt.
4. **American English** — Spelling and examples follow US usage.
5. **Phone-friendly** — Short sections you can read on a small screen.
6. **No teacher required** — Every activity can be done alone.
7. **Honest progress** — Small wins count. Compare yourself to yesterday, not to others.

---

## How to use answer keys

1. Always **try the exercise or quiz first**.
2. Open **[answer-keys/](answer-keys/)** only to **check** your work.
3. If you missed many answers, **review the module** — do not only memorize keys.
4. On a **shared phone**, move key files to a hidden folder or delete after checking.

## After you finish this roadmap

- Keep a **daily English habit** (even 15 minutes).
- Consume American content you enjoy (podcasts, subtitles, articles).
- Teach a sister or friend — teaching strengthens your skills.
- Use English for a goal: online course, job application, volunteer work, creative project.

You are building a skill that **no one can take from you**.
