## Checkpoint 1 practice (beginner)

1. Write 5 present tense sentences about your routine.  
2. Write 3 questions with *do/does*.  
3. Vocabulary: list 15 words on *home* from memory.  
4. Reading: answer questions 1–5 in [08-reading-drills.md](08-reading-drills.md) without looking back.  
5. Speaking: record 60-sec intro (self-score rubric in [14-assessments.md](../14-assessments.md)).

---

## Checkpoint 2 practice (intermediate)

6. Paragraph 100+ words: *A challenge I overcame* (past tense).  
7. Use *however* and *because* in that paragraph.  
8. Write 3 future sentences about next month.  
9. Dictation: use [06-listening-drills.md](06-listening-drills.md) #1–5.  
10. Speaking: 2-minute story recorded.

## Checkpoint 3 comprehension quiz
