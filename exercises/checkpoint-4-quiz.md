# Checkpoint 4 Quiz (Advanced review — Month 18–24)

**Time:** 75 minutes · Keys: [answer-keys/checkpoint-4-quiz-KEYS.md](../answer-keys/checkpoint-4-quiz-KEYS.md)

---

## Part A — Argument (1–5)

1. **Claim:** one sentence on education for women.  
2. **Evidence:** one fact or example.  
3. **Counter:** one opposing view.  
4. **Reply:** answer the counter.  
5. **Conclusion:** one sentence.

---

## Part B — Grammar (6–10)

6. Three passive sentences about learning English.  
7. Second conditional: If I ___ fluent now, I ___ (apply) for ___.  
8. Relative clause about a teacher or course.  
9. Formal rewrite: *Hey send me the link ASAP.*  
10. Informal vs formal: why use formal in job email?

---

## Part C — Nuance (11–14)

11. Use *although* in one sentence.  
12. Use *however* in one sentence.  
13. Hedge this: *All women must learn English.* →  
14. Name register difference: *kids* vs ___

---

## Part D — Writing (15–17)

15. Essay 120+ words: argument paragraph (claim + evidence + conclusion).  
16. Professional email 80+ words: follow-up after application.  
17. CV phrase: write 2 lines *Skills* and *Education* in English.

---

## Part E — Reflection (18–20)

18. Rate each skill 1–5 in [16-tracker.md](../16-tracker.md).  
19. Biggest win this year (English): ___  
20. Goal for next 6 months: ___

**Speaking:** Record 5-min presentation *My vision for my future* — rubric in 14-assessments.
